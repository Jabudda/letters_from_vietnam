Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,

Hi there. Today is Aug. 4 and all is well on the Eastern Front. So how all of you been. Just wonderful I hope. Oh, I just can't wait to get home. Its so darn close but yet so far. I'll be so happy. Got a letter from Jim yesterday. He too thinks we made a mistake coming back. I guess we're both just a bundle of nerves. These last few days are just full of suspense for me. Its so hard to explain. I know I'll get home but there always a doubt that lingers in my mind. All of us over here feel like that I guess. In a way I just took more cake then I could eat when I came back. But its [possible reading: nearer] to an end - Thank God. Never be able to thank him enough, and all of you. Prayer is the key word I guess. This place & even just the army taught me so many things. It was such a hard way to learn though. But that is probably what a bull headed guy like me needed, hay? I often ask myself - why me in a place like this? Its a stupid question I know. Cause I know why its me. Guess its just human nature asking questions hay? There's so many questions that are yet not answered though. Guess thats whats make the world go round. I just want to see and be with all of you again - for good. Talk, laugh, and even argue together. Mean the whole world to me. Can't wait. How's Petey doing? It'll be nice to watch him play and mainly ask me questions. Our family would have been so empty without him. Maybe someday [possible reading: Mike] or Petey will have to go through this or some other war. I just hope and pray not. God's going to be good to us and they never will. I just know it. At times I feel this world is in such a mess. But then I look at the good side and that over rules the bad side hay?

You know, I often wonder what heaven is really like. Guess someday I'll find out. Life is just too short. I don't know why I think so many things I do but you know me. Thats why I'm glad I have such a wonderful family who understands me.

Well gang, guess I'll go to bed for now and try to get some sleep. Sure can use it. Take good care of yourselves and be seeing all of you in the near future. [possible reading: Oh yeah], my port call is Aug. 17, at 11:00 at nite I'll be leaving for back to the U.S.A. I'll be leaving my unit about the 13th or 14th. Not long, thank God. Sure is hard to believe. Take care & God bless all of you from your ol' tired son who loves and misses all of you so much.

Love & miss all of you  
Tom
