Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,
    Hi there. Today is Aug. 4 and all [inserted: is]
well on the Eastern Front. So how all of
you been. Just wonderful I hope. Oh, I just
can't wait to get home. Its so darn close
but yet so far. I'll be so happy. Got a
letter from Jim yesterday. He too thinks we
made a mistake coming back. I guess we're both
just a bundle of nerves. These last few
days are just full of suspense for me. Its so
hard to explain. I know I'll get home but
there always a doubt that lingers in my
mind. All of us over here feel like that I guess.
In a way I just took more cake then I
could eat when I came back. But its [possible reading: nearer]
to an end - Thank God. Never be able to
thank him enough, and all of you. Prayer
is the key word I guess. This place & even
just the army taught me so many
things. It was such a hard way to
learn though. But that is probably what
a bull headed guy like me needed, hay? I
