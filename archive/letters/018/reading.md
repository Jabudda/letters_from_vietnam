*This folder holds two letters: a short one to his little brother Petey, in the envelope addressed to Petey, and a longer one to the whole family, dated July 9th.*

**To Petey**

Hi Petey,

Guess who this is. Your right its me - Tom.

Have you been a good boy lately? Better be - or no plane ride for you. Vellll??

Pretty soon you go up north. Are you going to go swimming with Mom. And fishing with Dad & Mike??

Pretty soon I come home right. Are you going to take me to the zoo. I'll be a good boy. Then we can go swimming fishing & hunting hay?

I'll miss your birthday Petey. Are you going to save me some cake? Sure hope so. I'm hungry. Or are you going to be a pig and eat it all by yourself?

Do you still take naps like a good boy? Pretty soon you will be too big for a nap.

Where am I going to sleep when I come home? On the floor or with you? Then we can go to your barber shop & buy a coke.

I have to go work now Petey so be a good boy OK. And give Dad, Mom, Kathy, Mary & Mike a big kiss & hug for me OK? Say your prayers that I come home safe and I'll pray that you be a good boy OK. By for now. Write soon.

Your brother  
Tommy

**To Dad, Mom & Kids**

Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,

Hi there. Well today is July [possible reading: 9th] and all is well. I'm back in Pleiku now cause our operation in the Dak To - Ben Het area is over with for the time being anyways. I have seen better days. Wrote a letter to Eddie Acoff's parents today. He died in the last attack we had up there. It was such a hard letter to write but felt I must cause knew he would have done the same for any of us. God seems so cruel at times but guess this a part of life many have to face. Such an ugly and sad part though. Its not the first and by no means the last. "Why" is a question I ask myself many times. I know the reason why but still ask myself that same question over & over again. Why can't the world live in peace? I only wish it could and perhaps some day we all will live in peace. We probably never will see it but maybe Petey's generation will. I just pray to God it won't be worse or even the same. One thing the Hippies do that is right is their peace movement. Only if the *whole* world felt the same and not just a few people. God's the only one who really knows the answer. Maybe someday we all will too.

It'll be so good to be home again. No lie, that's about all I can think of anymore is seeing all of you again ... for good. Today I wonder how many days I got. Time goes by so slow when one counts those days. That's why I like to let a few slide by once in awhile. Sounds crazy hay but you know me. Not crazy but happy that my time is near. It all seems so much like a dream though. I'll hate leaving all my friends behind but will do so. We must never stop hoping & praying for all those who are still here. Just because I'll be home doesn't mean that the war (conflict?) will be over. For me - yes - but not for all those who remain here. Right. It'll be one of the happiest days in my life for me to be home. But a happier one will be when this mess is over. I was hoping it would end while I was over here this time but it don't look like it. Oh well who knows what tomorrow may bring hay?

Well how was the vacation. Am anxiously awaiting to find out. Especially the fish stories. Did Petey ever get my letter? Somehow I think a few letters I wrote have been lost. Sure hope not though. Oh well, the VC NVA will never be able to read this mess anyways. And if they can - big deal hay? Maybe they'll return 'em. HA.

Well, guess I better get some sleep cause its after 11:30 (nite) and I'm beat. [unclear] take good care, God bless all of you & be seeing you all in the near future. Together again for good. By, by for now say "hi" to all. Thanks. Boy, it'll be so good to be home again for good. Just can't wait but don't have much of a choice in the matter hay? Such is life [unclear]!

Your son & brother  
Tom

Hope when I get home its in time for supper (BURP) or something. Either late or early I'll be just as happy.

See you soon but not soon enough - [unclear]
