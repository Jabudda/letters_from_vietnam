Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,

Hi there - today is April 8th and all is well. We got back to the hill yesterday. [possible reading: As] you know, we never left country yet. You should have gotten my last letter & pictures by now. I mailed them down in Cam Rahn Bay.

When I got back I got about 4 letters (1 from Dad) and a package. Sure did make me happy. Thats the only thing that did. Thanks alot for everything. I was so happy to hear from all of you again. Pretty soon I should be hearing from that school hay. Then we can get the ball rolling. Up until then I can't do nothing. So I hope they hurry up a bit. Then I'll be out of this place & the army also. And if not, I'll still get out of here but just a couple months later.

I'm writing this letter outside cause we have no lights for tonight. Its getting cool out already and its only 7:00. Pretty soon it'll be dark. I'd like to drop a line to Jim tonight but don't have much time. Oh well, maybe tomorrow if we get some time. And if not the next day.

There's alot of new guys coming in lately. It sure helps us out alot. Especially for guard. Now we're getting a whole 2 nights off - usually. All in all, things are pretty good as of lately.

I sure am tired again though. When I left it was not enough sleep now when I was there I got used to *too* much sleep. Just can't win hay? But it was worth it. Well gang guess I'll close for now and get some sleep. So take care of yourselves & God bless all of you from your son & brother who loves & misses you all so much.

Love & miss  
Tom

Sorry about the short letter but its getting dark. By for now. I'll write again first chance I get. - hopefully tomorrow.
