# Letter 023: notes

## Source

Two sheets of thin blue-grey airmail paper written in black ink, four pages. Only the third page is numbered ("3" in a circle). Addressed "Dear Dad, Mom & Kids" and dated in the text "Today is July 16th". Signed "Love & miss all of you, Tom", with a decorative flourish. Below, he wrote **"35 DAYS"** and circled it several times.

The plain envelope (flap open) is addressed in his hand to the "Jafuta Family" in West Allis, Wisconsin, with "Free" and the return address "Tom Jafuta, HAB [for HHB] 3/6 ARTY, APO SAN FRAN 96318". There is no postmark. **"7/16" is written on the front in blue ink in another hand**, matching the date in the letter (like "6/24" on Letter 010 and "6/13" on Letter 012). Only the front was scanned.

## Open questions for the family

1. **The year.** "July 16th", "35 DAYS", "30 some more days and I'll be home for good". Counting 35 days from 16 July gives 20 August, the same day his other countdowns reach (Letters 005, 010 and 011). So this is very likely **16 July 1969** (interpretation). It falls between Letter 018 (9 July, "back in Pleiku") and Letter 001 (21 July, "back in Pleiku with my unit"), which fits a two-day trip back from Phan Thiet. Confirm?
2. **John.** "John came up to our unit to pick up some things", and Dad went "down to John's place" in Phan Thiet. Is this the John of Letters 003 and 009 (in the hospital with a fever in May 1969)? Who was John, and what unit was he with?
3. **Kicked out of Saigon.** Dad and John "got kicked out of Saigon" for looking dirty. Did Dad ever tell this story?
4. **His eyes.** "They've been giving me trouble ever since I was in Ben Het." He wonders if the tear gas at Ben Het, when he had no mask, was the cause (Letter 005: "Don't worry, its just tear gas"). Did he have his eyes checked, and did the trouble last after the war?
5. **"Maybe you could call [unclear: a name, possibly 'Krugon'] and see what he has to say."** Who was this, perhaps the family doctor or eye doctor? Can anyone read the name?
6. **The vacation.** "So how was your vacation?" The same summer vacation as Letters 015 and 018 (West Bend, per Andy)?
7. **"7/16" on the envelope.** Who marked the envelopes with dates (Grandma?), and did they mark the date written or the date it arrived?
8. **Readings to check:** the name on page 3; "puss dries on them"; the crossed-out words on page 3; "By by for now".

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** "So here I am down in Phan Thiet. Its way, way down in the southern part along the coast of the China Sea." He went with John's help and his first sergeant's permission, "seeing how it's been pretty quiet lately", and expected the trip back to take about two days.
- **Historical context:** Phan Thiet, the capital of Binh Thuan province, is a fishing town on the South China Sea about 120 miles northeast of Saigon. LZ Betty, a few miles southwest of the town, was the home of Task Force South, which in 1969 directed U.S. operations in the four southern provinces of II Corps. *Sources:* [ichiban1.org, "The Attack on LZ Betty"](https://www.ichiban1.org/html/stories/story_35.htm); [Wikipedia, "Firebase Betty"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firebase_Betty).
- **Unknown:** Where John was based (the letter says only "John's place" in Phan Thiet). Nothing here places Dad at LZ Betty.
- **Verified fact (from the letter):** "When we got hit with Tear Gas up in Ben Het I didn't have a mask." This matches Letter 005, written at Dak To a month earlier.
- **Interpretation:** "35 DAYS" counts to about 20 August 1969, consistent with his ETS of 21 August (Letter 012) and his port call of 17 August (Letter 011).
