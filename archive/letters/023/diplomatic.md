Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,
    Hi there! Today is July 16th and all
is well. Sorry I haven't written
sooner but John came up to our
unit to pick up some things. So
I asked my 1st Sgt if I could go down
to John's place for a couple of days
seeing how it's been pretty quiet
lately. To my surprise he said
"OK stay out of trouble & be careful".
So here I am down in Phan Thiet. Its
way, way down in the southern part
along the coast of the China Sea. Its
not a bad place. Guess I'll leave to-
morrow cause it takes about 2 days to
get back. You'll never guess what
happened. John & I always wanted to
see Saigon so we stopped off there.
We looked like a couple of dirty country
boys in the "big city." It sure is
nice there but we got kicked out of

[page 2, not numbered]
Saigon believe it or not!! In Saigon
they all dress up with good clean uni-
forms and all. So we stood out like
a sore thumb. I guess those dumb
people there think all of Viet Nam is
like Saigon or something. Little do
they know. We were kinda angry
about being kicked out but decided to
leave peacefully. It still gets me
mad. It wasn't our fault that
we looked dirty but you know the
army. Such is life. At least now
when someone asks me if I was
ever in Saigon I can say "yes, for
a while".
    So how was your vacation? When
I get back to my unit guess I'll
find out through your letters. Pretty
soon we won't need any more letters
to communicate hay? Just good ol'
talking hay? Its been a long time
but an end is near, Thank God.
