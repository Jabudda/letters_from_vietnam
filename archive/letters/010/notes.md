# Letter 010: notes

## Source

Three sheets of thin airmail paper written on both sides in blue ink, six pages. Pages 2 to 5 are numbered in a circle at the top; pages 1 and 6 are not. Addressed "Dear Dad, Mom & Kids", dated in the text "Today is June 24th (I think)". Signed "Love & miss all of you, Tommy", with a decorative flourish below it, then a P.S. signed "Love, Tom".

The airmail envelope (red and blue border, printed "VIA AIR MAIL") is addressed in his hand to the "Jafuta Family" in West Allis, Wisconsin, with "Free" where a stamp would go and the return address "T. Jafuta, HHB 3/6 ARTY, APO SAN FRAN 96318". "6/24" is written on the front in darker ink, perhaps by the family when it arrived. There is no postmark. The back is blank.

## Open questions for the family

1. **The year.** He writes "At least for the next 57 days nothing is for sure", and is "still here in Dac To". Letter 005 (19 or 20 June 1969, confirmed) was written at Dak To with about 1,440 hours (60 days) to go. Four or five days later, 57 days to go fits exactly, so this letter is very likely **24 June 1969** (interpretation). Can the family confirm?
2. **Who wrote "6/24" on the envelope?** It is in darker ink than his address. Did Grandma or Grandpa mark the date the letter was written, or the day it arrived?
3. **Hawaii.** "Remember when we all went swimming in Hawaii. Or I should say Dad and I. At least you went in a little bit... Up to your knees wasn't it." Did Grandma and Grandpa meet him in Hawaii on his R&R? When was that, and are there photos?
4. **Sugar Camp Lake.** "Remember the one [the bonfire] at Sugar Camp Lake?" The family's usual "up north" was West Bend (Andy, 2026-11-10), so Sugar Camp Lake, much farther north in Oneida County, was likely a separate trip. Does anyone remember it?
5. **The movie camera letter.** He asks whether they got "the one where I sent prices about a movie camera... a bunch of pages taken out of a PX catalog". Letter 008 promised to send that PX page. Did those pages survive?
6. **"Up by A baty its a different story."** Does the family know anything about where A Battery was at that time? (See Historical notes; nothing here places Dad there.)
7. **Readings to check:** "their air strikes" (page 2, line 1); "Im writing alot" (page 3); the crossed-out word after "a little bit" and "knees" (page 4); "A good pile I bet" and "effected" (page 5); "& family" (page 6).

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** He is at Dak To ("We're still here in Dac To getting ammo out to a baty"), expects to go "back to Ben Het or Pleiku", and has 57 days left ("the next 57 days").
- **Verified fact (from Letter 005):** On 19 or 20 June 1969 he had arrived at Dak To by helicopter from Ben Het and counted about 1,440 hours (60 days) to go. This letter's "57 days" agrees with that count.
- **Verified fact (from the letter):** "Its been pretty quiet lately here in Dac To. Hope its because their air strikes are doing some good finally. Up by A baty its a different story though."
- **Historical context:** Ben Het was surrounded by North Vietnamese regiments from mid-June 1969 and the siege lifted in early July (see Letter 005 notes and sources). Cpl Harold F. Hawkins of A Battery, 3/6 Artillery, was wounded on 13 June 1969 and died on 18 June (Virtual Wall; see Letter 005 notes).
- **Interpretation:** "Up by A baty" probably means A Battery, 3/6 Artillery, which Letter 005 links to Fire Base Hawkins. The letter does not say where A Battery was, and nothing here places Dad with it.
- **Verified fact (from the letter):** He made a candle from wax melted in a can, first with a boot lace and then with a small rope for a wick, to write by.
- **Verified fact (from the letter):** "mail hasn't been going out much lately and worse yet none coming in."
- **Historical context:** US soldiers in Vietnam were entitled to a week of R&R outside Vietnam during a tour. Hawaii was a popular choice because it was close enough for family from the mainland to meet them, and the Army ran an R&R center at Fort DeRussy in Honolulu. *Sources:* [Australian Government, Department of Veterans' Affairs, "Rest and recreation in Sydney during Vietnam War"](https://anzacportal.dva.gov.au/wars-and-missions/vietnam-war/home/rest-and-recreation-in-sydney); [Marshall Independent, "The Vietnam War: Doug Hamilton, rest and recuperation" (2024)](https://www.marshallindependent.com/opinion/local-columns/2024/02/the-vietnam-war-doug-hamilton-rest-and-recuperation-and-return-to-vietnam).
- **Interpretation:** "We all went swimming in Hawaii. Or I should say Dad and I" suggests his parents met him in Hawaii, probably on R&R. Letter 008 mentions his R&R ("for R&R (before I went)"). When and where it happened is unknown.
- **Historical context:** Sugar Camp Lake is a 519-acre lake in the town of Sugar Camp, Oneida County, in northern Wisconsin, about 11.5 miles north-northeast of Rhinelander. *Sources:* [Wisconsin DNR, Sugar Camp Lake](https://apps.dnr.wi.gov/lakes/lakepages/LakeDetail.aspx?wbic=1020400); [Sugar Camp (community), Wisconsin (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar_Camp_(community),_Wisconsin).
