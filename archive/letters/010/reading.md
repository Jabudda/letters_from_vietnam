Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,

Hi there! Today is June 24th (I think) and all is well for me. We're still here in Dac To getting ammo out to a baty. I don't really know how much longer it will be. From here we either go back to Ben Het or Pleiku. Nothing is for sure. You know how the army is. At least for the next 57 days nothing is for sure. But after that. Well, we'll be together again and that is for sure. Isn't it! What a happy day that will be. Leading a normal happy life again. Its been pretty quiet lately here in Dac To. Hope its because [possible reading: their] air strikes are doing some good finally. Up by A baty its a different story though. Sooner or later things just got to get better. Don't they?

Guess what I did today? Found some wax and made a candle. Melted it in a can and used a boot lace for a wick. The wick didn't work so I had to re melt it and used a small rope. So it works so-so. Not the best but good enough to write a letter by - sorta. Nice & romantic hay? Opps its starting to get low again. Guess this wick isn't too good either but it beats a boot lace. Just tosed some paper in it to make it brighter. Probably end up burning the place down. No, not really.

Well, lets see what else is new. Nothing much really. Must admit, [possible reading: Im] writing alot these last few days. Just want to save you from worrying. Probably all the letters will get to you on one day anyways. Cause mail hasn't been going out much lately and worse yet none coming in. Things will probably get better in the next couple of weeks though. And if not - they got too on the 57th day hay? Or at least I hope so.

Boy, I bet the weather back home is really beautiful now hay. Warm breeze, clean air, and all. Oh well my day is coming, isn't it?? Can't wait to go swimming. Hope Petey learns to like the water. Remember when we all went swimming in Hawaii. Or I should say Dad and I. At least you went in a little bit. Up to your [possible reading: knees] wasn't it. Those were sure some happy & carefree days but just not long enough hay? Oh well, many more to come. Right? Right!

Well gang, guess I'll have to cut this here letter short cause this candle is giving me a hard time. If I keep tosing paper in it we'll have a bon fire soon. Remember the one at Sugar Camp Lake? Those sure are some happy memories hay. Many more to come too. Hay did you ever get that letter - the one where I sent prices about a movie camera? It had a bunch of pages taken out of a PX catalog? You never mentioned it - or is that another letter lost? At time I wonder how many of our letters get lost in the mail. A good [possible reading: pile] I bet. Especially packages. That can be [possible reading: effected] as of lately though with everything so mixed up and all. Oh well Things will get better. Sooo until I write again, take good care of yourselves & family, say "hi" to all & God bless all of you from your tired ol' son + brother who loves & misses all of you more then words could ever say. The gap is closing but it sure is hard to believe. Like a dream that may never come true - but this one will won't it? Well, by-by for now and God bless all of you.

Love & miss all of you  
Tommy
