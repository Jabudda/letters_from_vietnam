Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,

Hi there! Today is July 29th and all is well. It sure is good to see that this month is near an end. And then comes THE MONTH of August. It sure is hard to believe but its true hay. Gotta sorta rush this letter cause I got guard tonight. Boy, it sure has been cold, windy and of course raing out lately. Oh well it'll all come to an end. Every time I pull guard I just think - well here's one less guard left in the army. I got about 10 guards left.

Where am I going to sleep when I get home? Just curious. I'm glad the bed you bought is pretty hard cause I'm so used to sleeping on hard things that it will be just right. Thanks alot. Is Petey sleeping in my old bed? Where's he going to sleep when I'm home? Mike, Petey and me could all sleep in the same bedroom hay?

Hay, I *might* be going home Aug 18th now instead of the 21st. Thats what they told me so I'll check into it the first chance I get OK. This guy from one of our BTRY'S got med-evacked to Japan and he was suppose to leave Aug 18 so I'm suppose to take his port call. Get it? So when I find out *for sure* I'll let you know OK?

Glad to hear about the puppies. Sure hope we get one soon. Remember, pay for at least 1/2 out of my check OK? Thanks.

Well, I know this is a short letter but gotta get ready for guard. So will have to close for now. Take good care, God bless all of you and be seeing all of you in the *very* near future. By, by for now.

Love & miss all of you,
Tom
