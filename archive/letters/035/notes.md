# Letter 035: notes

## Source

One sheet of thin, blue-grey airmail paper written in black ink on both sides, two pages, not numbered. Page 1 runs straight on to page 2 ("sleep in the same bed- / room hay?"). Addressed "Dear Dad, Mom & Kids" and dated in the text "Today is July 29th". Signed "Love & miss all of you, Tom", with a flourish. At the foot of page 2, in another hand: **"20 DAYS or 23 days."**

The plain envelope is heavily foxed (brown spotting) and the flap is open. It is addressed in his hand to the "Jafuta Family" in West Allis, Wisconsin, with "Free" and the return address "T. Jafuta, HAB [for HHB] 3/6 ARTY, APO SAN FRAN 96318". There is no postmark. In pencil, in another hand, are "Jafuta" with a long stroke beside it; written sideways at the right, "19th of August / × after 3:00"; and on the open flap "Wolf's Dry Clean[ers] / 541…" (the rest hidden by the fold). Only the front was scanned.

## Open questions for the family

1. **The date.** "Today is July 29th." He expects to go home on 18 or 21 August, and his ETS was 21 August (Letter 026, 7 June 1969). So this is **29 July 1969** (interpretation, a strong one).
2. **"20 DAYS or 23 days."** Written at the foot of the letter in another hand. From 29 July, 20 days is 18 August and 23 days is 21 August, the two dates in the letter. Who at home counted the days? Grandma?
3. **"19th of August / after 3:00."** Pencilled sideways on the envelope in another hand. Was this the day and time Dad was expected home, or something else? Did he come home on 18 or 19 August 1969? (Andy has said Dad was out of the army before late 1970.)
4. **"Wolf's Dry Clean[ers] 541…"** On the flap, in pencil. A family errand written on whatever paper was at hand? (Not connected to the soldier "Wolf" in Letter 029.)
5. **The port call.** "This guy from one of our BTRY'S got med-evacked to Japan and he was suppose to leave Aug 18 so I'm suppose to take his port call." Did it happen?
6. **The puppies.** "Glad to hear about the puppies. Sure hope we get one soon. Remember, pay for at least 1/2 out of my check OK?" In Letter 025 (about 21 June) he asked for "a nice little hunting dog" and offered to pay half; in Letter 013 (6 August) Grandpa has a new pup and Dad again offers "to pay half". Was this the next Fritz?
7. **The bed.** "I'm glad the bed you bought is pretty hard… Is Petey sleeping in my old bed?… Mike, Petey and me could all sleep in the same bedroom." Where did the boys sleep when he got home?
8. **Readings to check:** "raing"; "HAB" on the envelope; "Wolf's Dry Clean[ers]" and the number on the flap.

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** On 29 July (1969, interpretation) he had "about 10 guards left", and he "might be going home Aug 18th now instead of the 21st", taking the port call of a man from one of the battalion's batteries who had been "med-evacked to Japan".
- **Historical context:** A "port call" was a soldier's assigned date and flight for leaving Vietnam; ETS is the end of his term of service (general knowledge; compare Letters 012 and 026).
- **Unknown:** Where he was when he wrote. Letter 018 (9 July) says the "operation in the Dak To - Ben Het area is over with", and Letter 023 (16 July) has him at Phan Thiet; this letter does not say.
