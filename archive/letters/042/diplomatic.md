Dear Mom,
  Hi there, Well today is Aug. 8th [possible reading: 9th, the figure written over] and
all is pretty well. Sorry for not writ-
ing sooner but we been working
some long hours lately. Also I been
running around getting my eyes checked
out too. Guess I'm leaving this unit
at a good time too. We've been pulling
guard every other nite too so for the
most part we stay tired and beat.
Soon I'll be home and then can
catch up on some much needed sleep.
Thats about all that keeps me
going over here is thinking of home.
I feel sorry for the guys who have
a long time left. But what can ya
do hay Mom? I just can't wait. 5 days
and a wake up left in good?? ol' 3/6 and
about 8 days left in the army. Sounds
good hay. But not good enough.

[page 2, a faint number at the top (possible reading: 2)]
Its been getting pretty active "again"
lately around here. I got a feeling its
going to be a bit worse then usual
lately mainly cause of that 6 week
quiet spell we had. They got something
up their sleeve, I bet. Oh well, not
many more days to sweat out hay
Mom? It sure is hard to believe but
its true.
  Ya know Mom, this is going to be
the last time I'll be "talking" to you
in a letter. In a way it sounds good
to say and in a way it makes me
feel kinda different. I guess cause its the
sudden change and also I find it hard
to believe its true. I mean I'm happy
and all but I'm so used to writing
that to stop all of the sudden makes
me feel different. Know what I mean.
Hope so - cause I don't know how to
explain it myself. When I hear your
voice on the phone though I'll be

[page 3, numbered "3" in a circle]
the happiest guy in the world. I
hope I don't cry though. I hate too
but at times can't help myself. I
know it sounds crazy for me but you
know me. Many people think that
2 yrs. over here has really hardened
me but I guess they don't know the
real me. I don't know the real me
at times either. This place has changed
me in ways I don't know how to de-
scribe [crossed out: unclear] but it hasn't hardened me. You
know what I mean. I just want to
go home and be with all of you.
  We sure have talked alot in letters
in the past 34 or so months hay Mom.
I'm glad though cause letters mean so
much to all of us over here, especially me.
Mainly cause I get so damn depressed
at times. We all do. I seen a picture in
the paper on a mother and father who
heard that their son, a POW was
released. The parents were just beam-

[page 4, numbered "4" in a circle]
ing with joy - you could see it in
the picture. It made me happy
just looking at it. Then again theres
others who have died here. I just
can't and don't want to "picture" their
faces at first hearing the news. How
did we get on this subject?
  Did you tell Petey that soon I'll be
home. Boy I can't wait to see how
he's grown. The first time I came
home he was sorta shy at first -
remember? This time I doubt if he
will be - do you. I'm kinda mad at
myself for forgetting his birthday but
there's been so much on my mind
lately. Oh well, I'll make up for
it hay.
  It still worries me a bit about
going back to school Mom. All these
sudden changes there'll be. I hope
it doesn't take long for me to get
the hang of things. Do you think

[page 5, numbered "5" in a circle]
it will? There I go again, asking
stupid questions. Probably cause I
learned to depend on you and the
rest of the family so much.
  Its hard to believe that this is
the last letter I'll be writing [crossed out: if] [possible reading: if]
things go as planned. In the army
nothing is for sure. Thats why you
shouldn't get worried if I don't call
on the date we planned. There's a 99%
chance I will but as I said nothing
is for sure. Right?
  Boy, its going to be nice to talk
to you in person Mom. Your really a
good person to talk too and I enjoy
every minute of it. So is Dad, and I
know we'll get along alot better
then before I came in the army.
This sure does change people alot
hay. I still got so much to learn
though. I guess we all do. Thats what

[page 6, numbered "6" in a circle]

Enuf of the many things anyways.
