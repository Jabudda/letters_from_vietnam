Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,
    Hi there! Today is April 24th and
all is well. I received your letter
of April 18 today and sure was
glad to hear from all of you.
    I still am driving for the rest
of this week. I thought it would
be for only a couple of days but
thought wrong! Such is life. I
still like to ride "shotgun"
better then driving. I love the
heck out of driving but over
here - just don't care for it too
much ... such is life. It will
all come to an end - hay??
I'm sure it will. A very
happy one too - right??
    I'm real glad ol' Petey said to
tell me he was being a "good

[page 2, numbered "2" in a circle]
boy". I guess maybe all of you
don't really know what it
means to me when you write
something he said about me or
too me. Like he looks up to
me or something like that. Know
what I mean? I kinda doubt
it but anyways - it sure brings
happiness to me. Just like all
the rest of you do. Boy, I can't
wait to get home. It'll be
so wonderful! Won't it?? Sure,
at times I have my doubts but
when I think of all of you, my
"attitude" changes completely.
    Its to bad Stevie had a
"relapse" again. His parents are
really something else. The more

[page 3, numbered "3" in a circle]
I hear about them the less
I think of them. That is all
"hear say" though. I just don't
know what to think of things.
    I know if something like that
happened to any one of us in our
family we would be side by
side. Not this coming the
next day sh---! I just couldn't
picture our family like that. It
just makes me sick. Please don't
read this part of the letter
about Stevie to anyone. I like
Steve & Kathleen alot but thank
the good Lord I'm not a son
of theirs. Stevie must be really
a mixed-up guy. Cause most people
I know would want to go
home when something bad
happened. I always did and

[page 4, numbered "4" in a circle]
still do. I've seen quite a few
guys cry for their families when
something bad happened since
I've been in the army. But again I
know there's many guys around
that don't have the wonderful
family such as I have.
    So Pat & Dennis are turkey
packers hay? History repeats
itself again! Remember when
Stevie & I worked there. I used
to enjoy it even though the
pay was lousy. Such is life.
    You keep telling me to tell you
the date of my discharge. I'd
like to but your the ones that
should know. Whatever month
I get accepted (will actually start
classes, I mean) is on or the
about date I will be dis-

[page 5, numbered "5" in a circle]
charged from the army. So as
soon as all of you know I
will know. I'm in such a place
where I can't see my C.O. all
the time and ask if he got a
letter from my college or not.
That's why I guess we're
getting things mixed up. Cause
I'm counting on you telling me and
your counting on me. Things
will [crossed out: to] work out for the best
I'm sure of. They usually do, maybe
not when planned but just
have time & patience is what I
think. When things go bad
I always think things
could be worse. Right?
    Well gang, guess I'll knock
off and get some "good"

[page 6, numbered "6" in a circle]
sleep. Each day that goes by
I get a bit more nervous.
I know why - cause I'm finally
nearing the END. What a
great day that will be.
    Sooooo take care of all
yourselves and God bless each
and everyone of you for me
who loves and misses you
all is more then a letter or
words can ever, ever, say.
                              Your son & brother
                                  Tom
