# Letter 019: notes

## Source

Four sheets of thin blue airmail paper written in blue ink. There are seven pages. Pages 2 to 6 are numbered in a circle; pages 1 and 7 are not. The letter is addressed "Dear Dad, Mom & Kids" and dated in the text "Today is April 24th". It is signed "Your son & brother, Tom" with a decorative flourish. A P.S. about Petey runs onto a last page, signed "Love & miss, T…" (the rest of that signature runs off the scan).

The envelope is printed "AFTER 5 DAYS RETURN TO … ZIP CODE" and is torn open along the top. Dad addressed it to the "Jafuta Family" in West Allis, Wisconsin. It has "Free" where a stamp would go and the return address "T. Jafuta, HHB 3/6 ARTY, APO SAN FRAN. 96318". There is no postmark. Only the front was scanned.

### Reading edition: changes from the diplomatic text

- Line breaks are joined into paragraphs, following his indented paragraph starts. Page markers and the envelope are kept in the diplomatic text only.
- "dis- / charged" (split at a line end) is joined.
- The passage about Stevie (end of page 2 to top of page 4), in which he asks, "Please don't read this part of the letter about Stevie to anyone," was at first held back. It is included now that the family has approved it (Stevie has passed away).

## Open questions for the family

1. **The year.** "April 24th". He is on his second tour, "finally nearing the END", and waiting to hear from his college before he gets a discharge date. Letter 009 (May 5) and Letter 008 (May 10) talk about the same school early-out. So this is very likely **24 April 1969** (interpretation). Confirm?
2. ~~**The Stevie passage (page 3).**~~ **Decided: include it.** Andy: "Stevie passed away so it is fine to include." (2026-11-10). Dad's own request not to read it aloud stays in the text as he wrote it.
3. **Who are Stevie, Steve and Kathleen?** Stevie was a friend who worked at the turkey farm with Dad ("Remember when Stevie & I worked there"). Is he the "Steve" of Letter 003, who was in Vietnam too? Are Steve and Kathleen his parents? Andy notes that Dad's sister Kathy was also called Kathleen. Here, though, Dad writes "I like Steve & Kathleen alot but thank the good Lord I'm not a son of theirs", which reads as if Steve and Kathleen are Stevie's parents rather than his sister (**interpretation**). Which Kathleen is this?
4. **Pat & Dennis, turkey packers.** "So Pat & Dennis are turkey packers hay? History repeats itself again!" This links to Letter 009, "the guys quit at my ol' turkey farm". Who are Pat and Dennis (brothers, friends)?
5. **His college.** "Whatever month I get accepted (will actually start classes, I mean) is on or the about date I will be discharged." Which college? (Letter 008 mentions "M.T.C.", probably Milwaukee Technical College.)
6. **Driving.** "I still am driving for the rest of this week… I still like to ride 'shotgun'." Does the family know what he drove? (A truck? Letter 010 has him "getting ammo out to a baty".)
7. **The plane ride.** "Tell him - pretty soon we'll go on a plane ride - if he's good." (See also the letter to Petey in Letter 018, "or no plane ride for you".) Did Dad and Petey go on that plane ride?

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** "I still am driving for the rest of this week… over here - just don't care for it too much." He hopes to be discharged when his college classes begin, and is waiting for word through his commanding officer.
- **Historical context:** In the Vietnam era, enlisted soldiers could be released early to enrol in college or vocational or technical school under a Department of Defense directive (DoD Directive 1332.15, reissued 5 January 1968). See Letters 008 and 009 notes and sources.
- **Historical context:** "Riding shotgun" means riding in the front passenger seat, originally as an armed guard. (General usage.)
- **Verified fact (from the letter):** "I've seen quite a few guys cry for their families when something bad happened since I've been in the army."
- **Unknown:** Where he was when he wrote ("I'm in such a place where I can't see my C.O. all the time").
- **Cross-reference (family, 2026-11-10):** Andy: "I think Stevie was Stevie Collins." He is very likely not the army friend Steve Dott of Letters 003, 037 and 039, whom Andy remembers as being from Tennessee (interpretation).
