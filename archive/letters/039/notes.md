# Letter 039: notes

## Source

Three sheets of lined notebook paper (blue rules), written in dark blue ink on one side, six pages. Page 2 and page 3 are numbered in circles and page 4 has a plain "4"; pages 1, 5 and 6 are not numbered. Addressed "Dear Dad, Mom & Kids" and dated in the text "today is May 13th". Signed "Your son & brother, Tom", with a flourish, and a P.S. for Petey signed "Love, Tom".

**Reading order.** The scans run 0204, 0205, 0206, 0207, 0208, 0209, but the text runs 0204 → 0208 (2) → 0205 (3) → 0206 (4) → 0207 → 0209: "If you / hear anything on Jim"; "asking favors tonight / and [possible reading: I've] been gambling"; "start my last / 90 day count"; "Pretty soon, / swimming weather"; "the gap / is closing finally".

The plain envelope is addressed in his hand to the "Jafuta Family" in West Allis, Wisconsin, with "Free" and the return address "T. Jafuta, HHB 3/6 ARTY, APO SAN FRAN 96318". There is no postmark. Only the front was scanned.

## Open questions for the family

1. **The date.** "today is May 13th"; his "last 90 day count" starts "about MAY 29th" and his discharge is "moved up to August 29th". Letter 003 (11 May 1969) is from the same days at the Oasis. So this is **13 May 1969** (interpretation, a strong one).
2. **Steve Dott (an answer to Letter 003).** On 11 May Dad feared "Steve [possible reading: Dott] may be worse off" and that "Steve's unit didn't know where he was" (Letter 003). On 12 May he "saw Steve Dott and he is as good as ever", and on 17 May he saw him again on patrol (Letter 037). Here the surname is written clearly, **Dott**, which supports the reading in Letters 003 and 037. "He goes home in August too (on leave)… ol' Steve decided not to extend after all." Andy (2026-11-10): "I think Steve Dott was from Tennessee and a good looking guy." Andy also remembers Dad telling of a friend who went home to Tennessee on leave and went hunting and fishing instead of chasing women. Was that Steve's August leave? Andy (2026-11-10): "That may have been on Steve Dott's August leave."
3. **The Harley (a cross-reference).** In Letter 009 (5 May 1969) Dad asked Grandpa to find out what it would cost to ship "a Harley 1200 c.c. from New York to Milw." Here, eight days later, he has given a friend $180 for "a '52, Harley 1200cc." from the friend's brother, to come to Milwaukee by freight train, and asks for a $180 money order to repay John. In Letter 025 (about 21 June) he writes "I also cancelled the deal on that cycle… You all were right all along." Andy has said Dad never bought the Harley. Did the money come back?
4. **Snoopy on 13 May.** "Snoopy is on the cot taking it easy. He know's how to stay dry." Snoopy was alive and with him in mid-May (see also Letter 027, 3 or 4 June, and Letter 025, about 21 June).
5. **The dates he looked forward to.** "In August I get out, in Sept. is your wedding anniversary, and in Oct. is Kathy's and Ed's big day." This confirms the September anniversary (Letter 026) and Kathy and Ed's October 1969 wedding (Letter 015).
6. **Discharge date.** "Moved up to August 29th." On 7 June (Letter 026) it moved again, to 21 August.
7. **John and Jim.** John lent him the $180 and was on guard that night (see Letters 023 and 027 for John). Jim: "haven't heard from him in so long" (see Letters 006, 008 and 036).
8. **Readings to check:** "[possible reading: smartened]" (page 1); "[possible reading: I've]" (page 3); "[possible reading: lived]" (page 3).

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** On 12 May (1969, interpretation) "we went out to the Oasis again", and they were to move "there, or near there" the next day. Letters 003 (11 May) and 037 (17 May) are from the same place and week.
- **Historical context:** Landing Zone Oasis, about 24 km southwest of Pleiku, was attacked on 10–11 May 1969. *Source:* [Wikipedia, "Landing Zone Oasis"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landing_Zone_Oasis).
- **Unknown:** Where he was when he wrote ("my area is flooded again").
