Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,

Hi there - today is May 13th and all is well - as usual. We went out to the Oasis again yesterday and I saw Steve Dott and he is as good as ever. Was real glad to see him too. He was so beat that I didn't stay and talk very long. We're suppose to move there, or near there - tomorrow so will probably see Dott again if we got some time. He goes home in August too (on leave) I guess ol' Steve decided not to extend after all. Guess he's [possible reading: smartened] up a bit. He'll be home on leave when I'll be out of the army.

I wonder how Jim's doing. Real good I hope but haven't heard from him in so long that I wonder. If you hear anything on Jim would you tell me - Thanks.

Oh yeah, I gave this real good friend of mine $180 to buy a cycle from his brother for me. Its a '52, Harley 1200cc. and is in good shape. His brother's a auto body worker & mechanic. Sooo, I'm having it shipped from New York to Milw. by freight (train) I doubt if it should cost very much. When you get a call from the Milw. train co. would you pick it up - sure would appreciate it. Also would you send a $180 money order to me. Cause I borrowed the money from John and owe it to him now. Gotta pay my debts off before I go home. I know I'm all full of asking favors tonight and [possible reading: I've] been gambling some money ($180) but you know me! If I loose, I'll loose and if I come out ahead - real good (which I believe I will). I just want something to work on and mess around with when I get home, - an "idle mind is the devils workshop" ya know.

John's on guard tonight - I think. It sure is wet out. How do I know? - mainly cause my area is flooded again. Snoopy is on the cot taking it easy. He know's how to stay dry. At times I wish I [possible reading: lived] a dogs life - mainly cause they get to sleep when they want!! Such is life, hay.

Well, about MAY 29th we can start my last 90 day count in the army. From then on, its all downhill. It sure feels funny at time I think its a life time and at time not. As you probably figured out my day of *discharge* (sounds good) has been moved up to August 29th. Boy, I can't wait!! Its so hard to believe at times. But it is coming to an end - isn't it?? I'd rather you count my days left then me in a way. Cause when I do, it drags by and I get nervous in a way. When August 29th rolls around though - I'll know my day has come hay.

Well, I'll bet its really getting warm out back home now. Pretty soon, swimming weather. But ol' Petey will love it as much as the rest of us. If not more. Hope I get some swimming in. But if not, still will be the happiest person - mainly cause we'll be one big happy family again. In August I get out, in Sept. is your wedding anniversary, and in Oct. is Kathy's and Ed's big day. What a good day to come home hay.

Well gang, guess I'll close for now and get some rest soooo take care of yourselves and God bless all of you from your ol' son & brother who loves & misses all of you is more then anything can ever say. Take care and the gap is closing finally (for good) It sure is hard to believe.

Your son & brother,
Tom

P.S. Give Petey a big kiss for me and tell him to be good - - or else ——. Say "hi" to all - - thanks.

Love
Tom
