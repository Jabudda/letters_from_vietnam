Dear Dad,
  Received your letter today
and sure was glad to hear
from you seeing how we
hadn't got any mail in such
a long time. Things have been
too mixed up lately. I know
alot of our mail is "lost." Maybe
things will get better soon, I
hope & pray. If not, these are going
to be a long 54 days (Aug. 21st). Ya
know Dad, I thought I seen very
bad times before but up in Ben Het
its terrible and unbelievable at
times. Even our BC from A baty broke
down 2 days ago. Right now I'm
still in Dak To helping to air lift
ammo & food to our baty. In a way
I'm glad & in a way not Dad. They
almost got over run 2 days ago again

[page 2, numbered in a circle on the fold (possible reading: 2)]
and suffered some bad casualties. A
good friend of mine (Acoff) died along
with others who got all messed
up. Now the casualties are up near
50 for A baty alone. Just can't win
up there. The NVA are dug in so
good that its even hard for air
strikes to get them! The only infantry
up there are the ARVN's and they
aren't worth a —. What we need
is some ROK (KOREANS) or american
infantry. They probably will get
there eventually but even a day is
too long off. I got a feeling these
Highlands are going to be like
they once used to be in '66 and
part of '67. Cause even the units
around Pleiku itself have been
getting hit frequently in the past
couple of months. Just 54 days left
Dad and I'll be out. Its still
such a long time to go through.

[page 3, numbered "2" in a circle]
Don't worry Dad, I'm not going
to sign up again. I doubt if I
could put up with even another
week past Aug. 21st. Guess this
country & army are finally starting
to get too me but not the best of
me. Too much will power I guess.
  I was hoping you'd mention some-
thing about the dog in this letter.
Oh well, maybe next one hay. We're
really going to have great times
together we I'm out, hay Dad? I
sure hope so and know so. It still
feels like only a dream though but
it isn't, is it. I bet you felt the
same way hay Dad. I mean when
you were in the army. Probably
everyone does hay.
  Well, the sun is starting to set
and another day is near a close.
I sure have been writing alot
lately and you probably know why
cause you know how it is when

[page 4, not numbered]

but I feel so much more "secure"
I guess you could call it that. A
good family is all a guy has
to hang on too.

  Our unit sent out some
new clothes to us today so
now I feel like a new person.
The last ones I had were about
to fall off and boy did (do) we
stink. Such is life though hay?
Remember when I told of the
CBS news guys & all. Well today
some NBC news guys were there

ammo & all. Big deal hay. Just

Real good I hope. Been working
