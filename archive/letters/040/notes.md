# Letter 040: notes

## Source

Three sheets of thin lined paper (faint blue rules), written in blue ink on one side, five pages. Pages 3 and 4 are numbered in circles; the others are not. Each page runs on from the one before ("the guards opened up / anyways"; "getting [possible reading: infused] to / another unit"; "then everything / piles into a few days"; "And a / few hours"). Addressed "Dear Dad, Mom & Kids" and dated in the text "Today is June 1st". Signed "Love & miss you, Tom".

The envelope is printed "AFTER 5 DAYS RETURN TO … ZIP CODE". It is addressed in his hand to the "Jafuta Family" in West Allis, Wisconsin, with "Free" and the return address "T. Jafuta, HHB 3/6 ARTY, APO SAN FRAN 96318". There is no postmark. Only the front was scanned.

## Open questions for the family

1. **The date.** "Today is June 1st"; John is to leave "Monday (tomorrow)"; "as of today I have 90 days left (at the most)"; discharge "August 29". 1 June 1969 was a Sunday, so the next day was Monday 2 June. So this is **1 June 1969** (verified by the weekday and the day count).
2. **The Buddha's Birthday cease-fire.** Dad's own account: guards on "the other side of our hill" saw movement in the wire, were refused clearance to fire because of the cease-fire, fired anyway, and "the next morning two bodies were found in the wire and blood trails leading back out into the woods". He adds that it was "the honest to God truth". Which hill he was on is not stated.
3. **Bobby.** "I sure hope Bobby has a job by now. Hope he gets the appoint." Andy (2026-11-10): "Appointment is correct." Who was Bobby, and what was the appointment?
4. **John leaves (a cross-reference).** "Ol' John is getting [possible reading: infused] to another unit and is suppose to leave Monday (tomorrow)… some closer then others, like John & me." In Letter 027 (3 or 4 June 1969) Dad writes that John had left. In Letter 023 (16 July) he is with John at Phan Thiet. John lent him $180 in Letter 039 (13 May). What was John's surname?
5. **Jim's address (an answer).** Dad asked for Jim's address in Letters 006, 036 (25 March 1969) and 039 (13 May). Here: "Sure was glad you sent Jim's address though cause I almost felt for sure something had happened to him. Thank God, not." Jim was all right in early June 1969.
6. **Early out and the phone call home.** "My early out was approved which moves the date up to August 29 (or a couple days before)… on the 29th you'll be hearing my voice… over the phone." Six days later (Letter 026, 7 June) the ETS moved again, to 21 August. Did he call from Washington State when he got back?
7. **"When I get to Wash. it takes about 16 hrs. to clear the army."** Washington State, very likely Fort Lewis (see Letter 036, "Ft. Louis") (interpretation).
8. **Readings to check:** "[possible reading: infused]" (page 2); the crossed-out words on pages 1 and 2.

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** Dated 1 June (1969); John was to leave for another unit on Monday 2 June; his early out had been approved, moving his date up to 29 August.
- **Historical context:** South Vietnam and its allies announced a 24-hour truce to mark the birth of the Buddha on 30 May 1969. *Source:* *University Daily Kansan*, 9 May 1969, p. 17 (wire report), [University of Kansas digital collection](https://digital.lib.ku.edu/islandora/object/ku-udk%3A73728/datastream/OCR/download). Dad's "just recently… our cease fire for Buddahs Birthday" fits that truce, two days before he wrote.
- **Interpretation:** The Army moved soldiers between units to stagger their departure dates ("infusion"); if the reading "infused" is right, Dad is using that term for John's transfer. Not checked against a source.
- **Unknown:** Where "our hill" was.
