Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,

Hi there! Today is June 1st and all is so-so. I also received your letter and a package with the cake in. It came in real good condition - BURP!

I sure hope Bobby has a job by now. Hope he gets the appoint.

You sure were right when you said how mixed up this war is. Some things I just find hard to believe even though I know their true. I could give alot of examples from just my own personal experience. Such as just recently with our cease fire for Buddahs Birthday. The other side of our hill the guards spotted movement in the wire (perimeter). So called and asked for clearance to fire. "No" was the answer because of the cease fire. Well, the guards opened up anyways. The next morning two bodies were found in the wire and blood trails leading back out into the woods. Sure you no doubt find that hard to believe but its the honest to God truth! If I was never here, I would find it hard to believe too. Ours isn't the only unit like that. A good share of our "leaders??" all over here are like that. Their the minority though, mainly cause theirs less Generals then colonels, less Col. then Maj. and so on. All the way down to us. My time is coming to an end finally and a happy guy I will be. I just hope it hurries up cause this place is making a nervous wreck out of me.

Ol' John is getting [possible reading: infused] to another unit and is suppose to leave Monday (tomorrow). That sure is too bad. Thats one of the many main reasons I can't use the army. One makes so many friends, some closer then others, like John & me. It never lasts though - I shouldn't say *never* but in most cases. When Jim gets out I know we'll see each other. And maybe John too. Sure was glad you sent Jim's address though cause I almost felt for sure something had happened to him. Thank God, not.

And I am sorry for not being able to write for the last few days but we been so damn busy. For a couple weeks its so nice and quiet - and at times even a month - then everything piles into a few days and then "peace?" again. Its so much of a waiting game that its nerve wracking at times. Many a time I wished it would happen just to get it over with. Its really weird I know. But the time is coming to an end again and this time for good. Yep, pretty soon the Day will come. Its about all I can think of anymore.

Good News, I just found out my early out was approved which moves the date up to August 29 (or a couple days before) Pretty good hay. So on the 29th you'll be hearing my voice and mainly I'll be hearing all of yours over the phone. And a few hours from that time we'll all be together. So as of today I have 90 days left (at the most) in this army. When I get to Wash. it takes about 16 hrs. to clear the army and then homeward bound. It sounds kinda funny talking like that cause I still don't believe its true - me getting out of here and the army I mean. But it is - isn't it??

Well, I guess I talked enough for today so will close this letter and try (or at least start) one off to Jim. So Dear family, take care, God bless all of you, say "hi" to all, and be seeing you in 90 days if God wants it that way. Once again take care & God bless all of you.

Love & miss you,
Tom
