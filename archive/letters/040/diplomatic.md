Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,
  Hi there! Today is June 1st and all is
so-so. I also received your letter and
a package with the cake in. It
came in real good condition - BURP!
  I sure hope Bobby has a [crossed out: unclear] job
by now. Hope he gets the appoint.
  You sure were right when you
said how mixed up this war is.
Some things I just find hard
to believe even though I know
their true. I could give alot of
examples from just my own personal
experience. Such as just recently
with our cease fire for Buddahs
Birthday. The other side of our hill
the guards spotted movement in
the wire (perimeter). So called and
asked for clearance to fire. "No" was
the answer because of the cease
fire. Well, the guards opened up

anyways. The next morning two
bodies were found in the wire and
blood trails leading back out into
the woods. Sure you no doubt
find that hard to believe but
its the honest to God truth!
If I was never here, I would find
it hard to believe too. Ours
isn't the only unit like that.
A good share of our "leaders??" all
over here are like that. Their the
minority though, mainly cause
theirs less Generals then colonels,
less Col. then Maj. and so on. All
the way down to us. [crossed out: unclear] My time
is coming to an end finally and
a happy guy I will be. I just hope
it hurries up cause this place
is making a nervous wreck out
of me.
