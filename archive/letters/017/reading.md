Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,

Hi there, I received your most appreciated package today and sure am thankful. Boy, I was hungry when it came. But once again - my good ol' family pulled through!

Oh yeah, I also received the check yesterday. Thanks alot for rushing it - now I can get out of hoc - for a while that is. Too bad it couldn't be forever but our time will come - won't it?

Boy, it sure is warm out tonight. Usually it gets real cool. You can tell it's before the monsoon season cause this is how it was the last time I was here. Nothing like a good ol' rain. On 3 months of it gets pretty monotonous. SP Fine anyways. Bet you would even like winter before this - hay? (I know how much you dislike winter!)

I just heard a song "I'm going home" or something like that. It sure sounded nice to hear - only wish that it was true. But someday it will be! - hay? *Boy* I can't wait to get home and see all of you again, - for good too!!! No more of this "leave" junk for us hay!? I can't wait. There's not many things I have looked more forward to then this.

Boy, I'm beat. I feel like taking some of the stay "awake pills" I have but will only use them on guard. Why waste 'em hay? Its just that I've been getting so darn tired lately. At times I feel like I could sleep for a year. A nice soft, clean bed is sure going to feel good. I just can't wait. There's so many times I dream I am home and never want to wake up. Of course, I do - but to what? - to this!! Time is going though - isn't it?

No, I can't say I really regret being here cause I don't. I hate it in a way but in another I don't. Sounds mixed up hay. I never would spend my life over here. But as long as I'm in the army I feel I should be doing something worth while. Not like I did in the state side army. As long as I know I'm accomplishing something - I'm happy in an odd sort of way. I'm sure [possible reading: Ted] (remember him) felt that way before he died. And many others I have known and thousands of others that I never knew. Life sure is a crazy mixed up thing when one gets right down to it, hay? I guess that's what makes the world go round.

When I go on R&R (if I still make it) I hope to call you just to say "hi" - on my bill of course. It sure will be wonderful just to hear all your voices again, even if it is just to say "hi" and all that goes with it. Its just that *every* time I call like from Hawaii I get tears in my eyes. Tears of happiness that is, - and of loneliness. Like when I landed in Hawaii (both times) and called you all. It never even dawned on me that I would cry. No I didn't just sit & cry but those ol' tears just rushed to my eyes. I guess no one will ever know what a family means to them until its gone. I never used to. It was always taken for granted - until we were apart. But the gap is closing hay.

I just hope that someday peace will come to the entire world. It sure would be great but the chances are slim. I'd like for to think that Mick & Petey will never have to do this but think they will. Hell, if its not here it will be somewhere else in the world we live in. I guess that's life though. Why just can't people discuss and compromise? - is what I always ask myself. Sure they are now but too me - that's a joke, a big one. If only Kennedy was still president. I'll bet we would either go all out or move out over here. That's the way it should be hay? I hate this waiting for things to happen. Whenever it does it's a great relief in a way.

Well gang, guess I'll close for now and get some sleep. So take care of yourselves and God bless each and every one of you from your son & brother who loves and misses you all so *very* much.

Love & miss you all  
Tom
