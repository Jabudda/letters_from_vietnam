## Open questions for the family

1. **The date.** Undated. Second tour ("this is how it was the last time I was here"), "before the monsoon season", R&R still ahead "(if I still make it)", and the family has just sent a package and a check. Probably **spring 1969**, before the May rains (interpretation). Does the family know more?
2. **Hawaii, "both times".** "Every time I call like from Hawaii I get tears in my eyes... Like when I landed in Hawaii (both times) and called you all." He landed in Hawaii twice and phoned home each time. Letter 010 remembers swimming in Hawaii with Grandma and Grandpa. Were those his R&R trips, or stops on the way to or from Vietnam? (Andy has added a photo, probably taken in Hawaii, to the media folder; who is in it and when are still to confirm.)
3. **"[Ted] (remember him)... before he died."** "I'm sure Ted (remember him) felt that way before he died." The name is uncertain (it could be "Ded" or another short name). Who was he? If he died in Vietnam, his name should only be published with the family's approval (see `docs/privacy.md`).
4. **"Stay 'awake pills'."** "I feel like taking some of the stay 'awake pills' I have but will only use them on guard." The family decides whether this line is published.
5. ~~**"Mick & Petey."**~~ Answered: Mick/Mike is his brother Michael; Petey is also his brother (Andy, 2026-11-10).
6. **"If only Kennedy was still president."** He means President Kennedy (interpretation). Did Dad talk about Kennedy at home?
7. **The song.** "I just heard a song 'I'm going home' or something like that." Does anyone know which song he meant? (Not identified here.)
8. **"Out of hoc."** He thanks them for "rushing" the check so he "can get out of hoc". Fine to publish?
9. **Readings to check:** "out of hoc" (page 1); "On 3 months of it" and the small "SP" above the line; "Ted"; "I'd like for to think"; "too me"; "If only Kennedy"; the crossed-out word after "I'll bet" (page 4).

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** He is on his second tour ("the last time I was here"), it is still before the monsoon, and he hopes to go on R&R and call home. He has "stay 'awake pills'" and pulls guard duty.
- **Verified fact (from the letter):** "No, I can't say I really regret being here cause I don't... as long as I'm in the army I feel I should be doing something worth while. Not like I did in the state side army."
- **Historical context:** The Central Highlands' rainy season runs from about May to October (see Letter 013 notes and sources). "Before the monsoon season" therefore points to a date before about May.
- **Historical context:** After the US halted the bombing of North Vietnam on 1 November 1968, the expanded Paris peace talks formally opened at the former Hotel Majestic on 18 January 1969. *Sources:* [Office of the Historian, US Department of State, FRUS 1964–68 vol. VII, doc. 286](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v07/d286); [FRUS 1964–68 vol. VII, preface](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v07/preface).
- **Interpretation:** "Why just can't people discuss and compromise?... Sure they are now but too me - that's a joke" very likely refers to the Paris peace talks, which were going on in 1969.
- **Interpretation:** "If only Kennedy was still president" most likely means President John F. Kennedy (died 1963). The letter says no more.
- **Unknown:** Where he was when he wrote.
