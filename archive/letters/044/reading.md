Dear Mom,

Received your much appreciated letter today (dated June 20th) and also a package the other day. That foot powder sure came in handy for all of us (3 now) cause the water was getting the best of 'em (our feet) and we only have 1 pair of socks. Some more should come in the next couple of days - although thats what they said last week too. But you know the army - hurry up & wait. Right?

As you probably read in my previous letter too that the phone call seemed like a dream the next day when I woke up. But it wasn't - was it. Sure am glad you didn't get a speeding ticket though even though it would have been worth it to hear your voice again. Yes, I did hear you pretty good - real good for being such a long distance & in the middle of almost no where. It sure was nice.

Well in about a couple more days or so I'll be in the 40's - not my age but 40 days that is. The exact amount of days I don't know off hand cause we been working hard the last couple of days.

Today some NBC news men were taking pictures of us air lifting ammo so like I told Dad, if you see a familiar face on the news guess who? I'm for CBS myself. Yeah, finally got in the movies. CBS & NBC. Maybe next Hollywood who knows - HA! Cartoons maybe.

Boy Mom, it will be so damn nice to be home again - for good that is. I just can't wait. Especially since we been up in Ben Het. Oh, you asked where Ben Het is. Just a small place 7 mile WEST from Dak To here. So close but yet so far. Its really bad up there now Mom & I sure worry about Deacon & all the rest up there. There's probably a big thing about it back home. But there's more to it then meets the eye. I'll probably explain that to you when I get home. I don't know what the paper & news say about it back home & don't really care as long as you know I'm OK. Thats why I have been writing so much lately. Cause to stop your worrying. I worry enough to make up for a 100 people. Not just for myself but all of my friends. I guess you could say that I feel a part of this unit more so then others cause almost 2 yrs. is a long time. We've had many good times and alot of bad ones. And when I leave 3/6 it will always be in my memories no matter how hard I try not to think of some of the things. I wish I could just think of the good times but that too is impossible, Mom. No, I'm not going to be messed up in the head or anything cause I took the step and learned much about life - a different type of way then most people do - or I should say the average person. Proud?? Yes I am - proud of my family for sticking with me. And for once in my life I am fairly happy with myself cause of the help I gave to these people in this messed up country. Sure it wasn't much but it was some hay. Most of it is because of the wonderful family I have & love so much. If I wasn't brought up the way I was, well at times I often wonder where I'd be now. This place has changed my thinking (for the better) in so many ways. Used to take life for granted Mom. But not anymore. Not since I learned how easy a person could die. There's many other things but it all points back to you & Dad. Of course there's always room for improvement for me - that & advice. You & Dad will be one of the first I'll go to for advice in certain things. No, I'm not going to be a no-it-all like I once used to be.

Boy Mom, its just going to be so nice to get home with all of you. We'll all be a happy family again. Mainly cause you & Dad made it so. After all we're your kids, thank God.

Well Mom, gotta go for now & get some sleep. Take good care of yourself, have a good vacation and be seeing you in the *near* future - thank God. It still feels all like a dream Mom but it isn't - is it. God bless one of the best Mothers in the world - mine. Good night for now. 1 day less.

Your ol' TIRED TOO son,
Tommy

P.S. I wrote a letter to Petey too. How's he like it?
