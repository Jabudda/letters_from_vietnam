# Letter 027: notes

## Source

Three sheets of thin airmail paper written in blue ink, five pages. Pages 2, 3 and 4 are numbered in circles; pages 1 and 5 are not. Each page runs on from the one before. Addressed "Dear Dad, Mom & Kids" and dated in the text "Today is June 3rd (or 4th)". Signed "Love & miss all of you, Tom", with a decorative flourish. A rust-brown smear crosses the middle of page 4; the writing under it is still legible.

The envelope is printed "AFTER 5 DAYS RETURN TO … ZIP CODE" over ruled lines. Dad addressed it to the "Jafuta Family" in West Allis, Wisconsin, with "Free" and the return address "Tom Jafuta, HHB [possible reading: 3/6] ARTY, APO SAN FRAN 96318". There is no postmark. Only the front was scanned.

### Reading edition: changes from the diplomatic text

- Line breaks joined into paragraphs, following his indented paragraph starts. Page markers and the envelope are kept in the diplomatic text only.
- Words split at a line end joined: "go- / ing", "com- / ing".
- Crossed-out words and the flourish under his signature are left out; both are in the diplomatic text.
- His spelling, grammar and punctuation are unchanged, including "Make any sense.", "everyones", "Eds", "John is left", "Hope he makes it OK?", "these people don't believe in it", "too" for "to", "then" for "than", "Soooo", "By-by" and "hay".

## Open questions for the family

1. **The year.** "June 3rd (or 4th)". He still counts to **29 August** ("the month after next I'll be home"). Four days later, in Letter 026 (7 June), he reports that his ETS "has been moved up to the 21st of August instead of the 29th". So this letter is very likely **3 or 4 June 1969**, just before that news (interpretation). Confirm?
2. **The family calendar, in his words.** "Next month all of you go on vacation… And the month after that I come home, the following month your wedding anniversary & the month following that is Kathy & Eds big day (and also pheasant season, hay Dad)". So: vacation in July, home in August, Grandma and Grandpa's anniversary in September (as Letter 026 also says), and Kathy and Ed's wedding in October (as Letter 015 says). Can the family confirm the dates?
3. **The Dells on his cycle.** "Remember when I went up to the Dells on my cycle to see you… To tell the truth I was really surprised to find you." So Dad had a motorcycle before the army, and once rode to Wisconsin Dells to surprise the family on vacation. When was that, and what kind of cycle? (The Harley 1200 of Letter 009 was a later plan, cancelled in Letter 025.)
4. **John.** "John is left… We used to just sit around and talk for hours and now he's gone… he'll be coming up to Milw. for awhile when he gets out in Sept. John knows a girl in Union Grove, Wis." This is very likely the John of Letters 003 and 009 (in the hospital in May 1969), and the John whom Dad visited "down in Phan Thiet" in July (Letter 023) (interpretation). Did John visit Milwaukee in the fall of 1969? What was his surname?
5. **Snoopy.** "Snoopy just came in all full of mud. And the first thing he did was jump on my cot." Snoopy was with him in early June 1969 (see Letters 002, 008, 016). Andy (2026-11-10): "Dad's Vietnam dog was Snoopy, but he may have been KIA." See Letter 025.
6. **"I guess these people don't believe in it though."** About sleeping on a rainy day; "these people" seems to mean the army (interpretation).
7. **Readings to check:** the unit on the envelope ("3/6", written oddly); "John is left"; "[possible reading: humans anymore]" (page 4); the crossed-out words on pages 4 and 5.

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** "The monsoons are here." "Its still raining out… All guys got wet & muddy today."
- **Historical context:** In Vietnam's Central Highlands the rainy season runs roughly from May to October, with the heaviest rain in the summer months (see Letter 013 notes and sources on the Pleiku climate).
- **Verified fact (from the letter):** In early June he still counted to 29 August as his day home.
- **Unknown:** Where he was when he wrote.
