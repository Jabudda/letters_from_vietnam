Dear Kathy,

Hi there! Was just laying here thinking who to write and thought of you. Seeing how I *think* your birthday is this month sometime. Well, is it? If so Happy Birthday. And if not it will either be a late or early Happy Birthday greeting from me. Make sense?? So how ya been lately. Real good I hope. How's ol' Eddy boy doing? Say "hi" to him for me.

Well, by the time you get this letter I'll (or should be) in the 40 some day bracket. 1 month & days. Sure is hard to believe that its coming to an end for me. But it is - no two ways about it hay? Im sure glad I'll be home for your wedding. Is it still in October?? You ought move it up too Mom & Dads wedding anniversary then we could have a hell of a party hay. Oh well, maybe 2 separate parties are better then one anyways hay. Parties aren't everything though. I just hope when The Day comes you & Ed are taking the right step. Ed sure is a good guy from what I've seen better then all your other old "flames" so guess there's not much to worry about. It should be for the most part a good happy marriage. Right? Right!

So what you been up to lately. Working double shifts and all. Ed sure is a hard worker. I couldn't see myself working double shifts - must be lazy hay. Did you decide where your moving too yet? Probably an apartment huh? You can always use my bed room for 50¢ a week. I'll just dig a hole outside & sleep in it. Gets kind of comfortable after being here so long.

Boy, bet the weather back home sure is nice about now hay. Are ya going on vacation with the rest of the family? Sure wish I could make it but thats the way it goes. "So close but yet so far." Thats what it feels like here for the next 50 some days. Back home 50 days would be a drop in a bucket but over here it feels like a life time & more. But a day is a day no matter where you are hay. So how's work been lately? Are you & Ed still going to work there after your married? Just curious - as usual. Still full of questions but you know me. Maybe if I ask alot of questions you'll write back sooner hay. HINT.

Boy, do I stink. Sure could use a shower & shave but that will have to wait. If I came home like this I'd probably stink everyone out of our neighborhood. Hay then you could have your choice of houses - something to think about. But its really fun to be watching Petey grow up hay? He sure is a neat lil' kid and I can't wait to see him and all of you again. What a happy Day that will be.

Well Kathy guess I'll close for now and dig up something to eat. BURP. Soo take good care of yourself, write, & be seeing you in the near future. By for now. Say "hi" to Ed & all & give Petey a big hug for me - thanks.

Your ol' brother  
Tom

P.S. HAPPY BIRTHDAY (I HOPE)
