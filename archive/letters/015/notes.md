# Letter 015: notes

## Source

Three sheets of thin airmail paper written in blue ink, five pages. The second page is numbered "II" and the fourth "III" at the top; the others are not numbered. The letter is addressed "Dear Kathy" and has no date. It is signed "Your ol' brother, Tom", with a decorative flourish below it, and ends with a P.S. in capitals: "HAPPY BIRTHDAY (I HOPE)".

The reading order is 0073_b, 0074_b (II), 0074_a (the back of II), 0075_a (III), 0075_b. Each page runs on from the one before ("Im sure glad I'll / be home for your wedding"; "Working double shifts / and all"; "over here it feels / like a life time & more").

The airmail envelope is addressed in his hand to "Kathy Jafuta" in West Allis, Wisconsin. It has "Free" where a stamp would go and the return address "T. Jafuta, HHB 3/6 ARTY, APO SAN FRAN 96318". There is no postmark, and the back is blank.

## Open questions for the family

1. **Kathy and Ed.** Kathy is his sister ("Your ol' brother"), engaged to Ed ("Eddy boy"), with the wedding planned for October. Andy confirms Kathy is Dad's sister and Ed her husband; their reception was at Polaski Hall. She was also called Kathleen (Andy, 2026-11-10). Did the wedding take place in October 1969, and did Dad make it home for it? May Kathy's and Ed's names appear in the book?
2. **The date.** The letter is undated. "By the time you get this letter I'll (or should be) in the 40 some day bracket" and "the next 50 some days" suggest late June 1969: Letter 010 counts 57 days to go on 24 June. Her birthday is "this month sometime". So this is about June 1969 if her birthday is in June, or early July if it falls then. When is Kathy's birthday? (Interpretation.)
3. **Mom and Dad's wedding anniversary.** "You ought move it up too Mom & Dads wedding anniversary." That suggests his parents' anniversary fell in or near October. Is that right? It is only needed if the family wants it in the book.
4. **The family vacation.** "Are ya going on vacation with the rest of the family?" Andy says "up north" usually meant West Bend. Was this the family's trip to West Bend?
5. **Working double shifts.** Where did Kathy and Ed work ("Are you & Ed still going to work there after your married?")?
6. **Readings to check:** "Seeing how I think" (page 1); "You ought move it up too" (page 2); "BURP" (last page).
