# Letter 032: notes

## Source

Two sheets of three-hole lined notebook paper (red margin, blue rules), written in black ink on both sides, four pages, not numbered. Each page runs on from the one before. Addressed "Dear Dad, Mom, & Kids" and dated in the text **"Today is Jan 17th, 1969 (of course)"**. Signed "Your son & ol' brother, Tom", with a decorative flourish, and two postscripts.

The plain envelope (torn at the left) is addressed in his hand to the "Jafuta Family" in West Allis, Wisconsin, with "Free" and the return address "Tom Jafuta, HHB 3/6 ARTY, APO SAN FRAN 96318". There is no postmark. Only the front was scanned.

## Open questions for the family

1. **The date (written in full).** "Jan 17th, 1969 (of course)". It falls between Letter 004 (15 January 1969) and Letter 002 (22 January 1969).
2. **"That one letter."** "I must have really been in a depressed mood that day I wrote that one letter." Letter 004, written two days earlier, says he was "feeling depressed" and calls the army "a legalized racket". This is very likely the letter he meant (interpretation).
3. **The Oasis.** "I think I'll be going back out to the Oasis tomorrow." Letter 002 (22 January 1969) has him "out at the Oasis for 3 or 4 days". So he very likely went out on 18 January (interpretation).
4. **Kathy's money order.** "Did Kathy get that money order I sent… Don't forget to tell me when she gets it." Letter 004 asks the same about "Kathy… that $120 money order".
5. **M.T.C.** "I want to take math plus a bit of business law… that secretarial course was the closest thing to it… If you could change it I'd be glad." And: "did you check to see if I could get a letter of acceptance cause taking the A.C.T. is kinda hard to do right now." Milwaukee Technical College (see Letter 008). Did he enrol after the army, and in what?
6. **Jim's house.** "So you had a good time at Jim's house hay?" The family visited Jim Theilke's family? (Letter 022 suggested calling Jim's mother.)
7. **Petey "powed" again.** The same word as Letter 024 ("Did you get powed lately??"). A family word for a spanking?
8. **The projector and the camera.** "Sure am glad you like the projector." He had sent a projector home, found the movie camera "too big and clumsy", and wanted "one of those little 35mm cameras". Do the projector, or any slides or films, survive? (See also Letter 008 on the Super 8 movie camera.)
9. **$200 from his pay.** "Near the end of the month - I mean the beginning of the next - you should get a $200 which they are now taking out of my pay check." The start of the $200 monthly allotment mentioned in Letters 008 and 029. Fine to publish?
10. **Readings to check:** "right [possible reading: here] / hope so" (pages 1–2); "lil' ol'"; "60 (machine gun)".

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** Dated 17 January 1969. "Had to fix a line before. It seems everytime a line goes out its at night." (Compare Letter 004, where he "fixed a bunker line and got a shock".)
- **Verified fact (from the letter):** "They killed a big ol' tiger out there [at the Oasis] about 3 days ago." A snake "about 5' to 6' feet long" was shot with "a 60 (machine gun)" and given to villagers who ate them. These are his reports.
- **Historical context:** The M60 was the standard U.S. general-purpose machine gun in Vietnam; soldiers called it "the 60". (General knowledge.)
- **Historical context:** LZ Oasis was a base about 24 km southwest of Pleiku (see Letters 002 and 003 notes and sources).
- **Unknown:** Where he was when he wrote (very likely his unit's base near Pleiku; interpretation).
