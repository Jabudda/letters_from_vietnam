Dear Dad, Mom & Kids

Hi there! Today is June 18th and all is pretty good on this end of the world. How's things on your end? Real good I hope. We're still up in Ben Het yet. And the word is in the next few days we're suppose to move to Dak To for a couple week and then back to Pleiku. One can't believe nothing until the time comes though. I'll be happy if its true though. Cause Dak To is a bit more better then here. Oh well, every day is a day less and thats about the only way I can look at it. Must be at least down in the low 60's (days) today. I like to let a few days go by before I make the exact count. Seems to make time go by a bit faster. The days out here pretty much drag by. Mainly cause we're hold up and there's not much to do except for things that keep us operational. And that we have to do at night time. Such is life hay. It'll all come to a happy end hay. Boy it sure will be a happy day to get out of this country and army too! What a day. But for the time being it seems just like a great dream that will never come true. But it will - [possible reading: won't it]. Boy it sure is hot & humid out today. Lousy weather! Wonder how the weather is back home. Real nice I bet. Am I right? Pretty soon you'll be on vacation in sunny Wisconsin hay? Sure would be nice if I could tag along with all of you. Oh well, next summer for sure hay? I sure hope the weather stays nice for your vacation. Just had to figure it out. Today is [possible reading: 63] days left in the army at the mostest. Still feels like a life time. Actually 62 days left if we don't count today which is almost over anyways. Right? Right!

Boy, a cold shower would feel good. Hope if we make it to Dak To they have plenty of water. Its rationed a helmet full a day which is hardly enough to take a shower with. For 62 days I could get by without a shower at all. If we get a chance though I'll take it. (a shower that is!)

Deacon and I are living in this old French bunker down near the perimeter. We found it the other day and decided to move in cause it looks much safer then our own. There's only one catch, it floods [possible reading: easy] which we found out the hard way. We couldn't do nothing but laugh. We built the floor up a bit so I hope it stays a little dry. Can't win 'em all hay? What we need is a good plumber - hay Dad?

Well guess I should get down there and see how it is cause its pouring out now. Right now we're in a friends' of ours bunker writing letters cause he's the only guy with dry paper to write on. So dear family, guess I'll close for now. The gap is closing now. I just wish I could fall asleep and wake up and be home. But thats just daydreaming which we sure do alot of. Soooo take good care of yourselves God bless all of you and be seeing you all in the near future. Then we'll be one big happy family again hay? Boy, I sure do miss all of you so much and it seems like I'll never get home but time has a way of passing hay. Thats what you once said hay Mom? So until my next letter take care & God bless.

Your son & brother,
Tom
