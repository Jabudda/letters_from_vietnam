Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,
    Hi there, just got all kinds
of mail today. One from Mom, 1 from
Dad, one from Tom [possible reading: Kulinski], one
from John [possible reading: Danbeck] & one from Tim.
I think thats the most I got
at one time since I been back here.
    To me that letter from M.T.C.
didn't look like a letter of accept-
ance. Thats why I sent it to
you to find out what's what.
I sure hope its one though. Oh
well soon we'll find out hay.
    That sure made me happy
that Petey remembers me buying
him a soda at Jack's. We haven't
spent much time together since
he was born. Thats why
I'm so glad he remembers
most we did together. Their

[page 2, not numbered]
small things to many people
but too me it means an
awful lot.
    It must finally be warming
up outside hay. I'm sure glad
of that for all of yours sake. It's
been getting cold out here at
night lately - especially on guard.
I got it tonight by the way.
Oh well, I don't mind - too
much.
    Glad to hear Chuck made out
Ok on the car. I was afraid
he'd loose out on the deal.
I sure hope that Chuckie sticks
out high school. They really
should make that part of his
probation. Chuck should check
into that. High school is
really important. Ol' Chuckie just
doesn't realize that. He will

[page 3, numbered "3" in a circle]
someday though - the hard way
from the way it looks.
    I'm real glad to hear that Mary
got a job at D.Q. Maybe when
I get home I'll be able
to get a _free_ hamburger &
malt or something. Hay Mary?
Know you would "smuggle" me
one. Nothing like a good reliable
family which I have and am
so proud off. No one really
knows except ol' me and you.
    I just cleaned my rifle. We went
out to Blackhawk yesterday to
escort a "low boy" with a 155
gun in. It sure was a dusty
ride. One of the guys on the gun
was from 35th and Capitol. The
lucky guy has 30 some days
left. But a year & 1/2 to serve
in the states. So I really don't

[page 4, numbered "4" in a circle]
consider him too lucky. Our B.C.
got mad at us cause we broke
the window on the jeep. We
had the windshield turned
down with the machine gun
on it (on top of 2 sand bags) but
the 60 turned over and broke
the window. Oh well, we don't
need a windshield anyways.
When the monsoons come we'll
regret it but until then
big deal. Boy, I can't wait
to get out of this army and get
home - where I belong. It sure
is going to be nice - carpeted
bedroom and all.
    Hay, it's about time to eat.
I wonder what kind of "garbage"
we'll have today. Hope its
good - HA. Nothing like home

[page 5, numbered "5" in a circle]
cooked meals. I can't wait.
    I'm glad to hear you all liked
[possible reading: Snoppy]. What did Petey say?
Of course its was good hay?
    Yesterday when we came back
from Black hawk (near An Khe) we
stopped at a village to watch
a Buddist [he wrote "sp" above the word] ceremony - the out
door type. It sure is a weird
ceremony. I wish I had a
camera. Oh well, soon I'll get
some pictures. Just take some
money out of my check and get
the kind I asked for Ok? Thanks.
    Well gang, guess I'll close
for now and go eat & get
ready for guard and do some
work. So dear family, take
care of yourselves and God bless
all of you. I'm sorry about some
of my letters but I write

[page 6, not numbered]
how I feel and when depressed
I write bad letters. But one
thing I'd like to stress is that
I don't ever feel sorry for myself.
I'd like too at times but know
its best and useless too. So
once again, take good care
and always remember I love
and miss and need all of you
very, very, much.
                                  Your son & brother
                                    Tom
                                    [a decorative flourish below the signature]

P.S. Say "hi" to all - Thanks.
