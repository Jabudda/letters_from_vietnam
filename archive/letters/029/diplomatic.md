[page 1, USO letterhead; at the top, in pencil, in another hand: "EASTER" (underlined) and "APRIL 6, 1969"]
Dear Dad, Mom & Kids
    Well here I am, still down in Cam
Rhan Bay. We've been down here
since the 31st and haven't seen
Australia lately. We finally gave up
yesterday and are now awaiting to
get back to our unit. Boy, what
a let down hay? Oh well, such
is life. At least I got plenty
of rest down here so really ain't
too bad off. We even went
swimming down by the ocean al-
most every day. I'll send some
pictures of that to you all.

[page 2, USO letterhead; numbered "2" in a circle]
The water sure was warm
and salty. This is the cleanist
I've been since my last R&R. I
really wanted to get out of
this country bad but should
be happy with what I got
hay? We seen a couple guys
from our unit down here going
on R&R. They left after us by
about 4 days. The hill got hit
again as we were told. Sure
hope ol' Snoopy [written like "Snappy"; the family confirms the dog was Snoopy] is OK, &
everyone else of course.
    The reason I prolonged writ-
ing was in hopes of leaving

[page 3, USO letterhead; numbered "3" in a circle]
here so I could personally call
and wish you all a Happy Easter.
But things didn't go as planned
- as usual. Today is Easter so
a late but Happy Easter to all
of you from me who loves
and misses you all so much.
I would of wrote sooner if I
knew this was going to happen.
But you know me, always hop-
ing for the best and never giv-
ing up. But now I have too.
I sure would have loved to
hear all your voices again
I was really looking forward

[page 4, USO letterhead; numbered "4" in a circle]
to that and other things. Oh
well, the next time I talk
to you I'll be a real live civilian
sounds good hay? No sense feeling
sorry for myself. Right? Right!!
Boy, it sure is hot down here.
I'm glad its nice & secure down
here cause we (John & I) sure
got some nice peaceful sleep. I
feel like a new person - almost.
I'll be sending this money
home the first chance I get.
Did you get the last $200 yet?
From March's check. Sure hope
so.

[page 5, USO letterhead, front of the third sheet; "OVER" at the foot]
How's my school early out paper
coming. Hope you got that one
letter telling what the school
would have to write for my
early out. If I can get that
letter pretty soon, things should
be all set. It has to be
phrased about in the same
words as the copy I sent you.
The copy I sent was to give
you & them a general idea
of how the letter must be.
Maybe it's back on the hill
waiting for me already - sure
hope so. Then we could
get the ol' ball rolling and
                                              OVER

[page 6, back of the third sheet]
keep our fingers crossed. I
could get out between June &
Sept. (90 days early) If June, thats
the month after next - sure
sounds good hay? Oh well, soon
we'll find out - or I will.
    So how's the weather back
home? Really getting nice I bet.
If things go right I could be
home for summer. Then Petey &
I could go for a plane ride.
If he's a good boy. Does he
still remember about the plane
ride. Did you tell him I said
he has to be a good boy in order
to go. What did he say about
that? Boy, I can't wait to get
home. Thats all I can think
about anymore. Being a civilian
and especially being home with
my family and friends. One really

[page 7, USO letterhead, front of the fourth sheet]
learns how to appreciate the smallest
things in life after being over here.
Last year at this time I was
home on leave from over here.
About the same time as when Brunner
Kruse & Wolf got killed. And before
that it was Del and many others
- especially during Tet of last year.
I sure hope & pray they end this
mess so others will never have
to come here. But thats just
wishful thinking hay? Maybe
not though. Who knows
what tomorrow will bring
hay? Well gang, guess I'll

[page 8, back of the fourth sheet]
close for now and go back on
my flight in hopes of getting
back to the unit today. So take
care of yourselves and God bless
all of you from your ol' son
& brother who loves & misses
you all so darn much. Happy
Easter again. Better late then
never hay?
                                  Love & miss all of you
                                    Tom
                                    [a decorative flourish below the signature]

P.S. Say "hi" to all for me.
P.S. Did Petey find his Easter
basket. I can just picture him
and all of you now sitting around
having the annual Easter dinner.
Boy, wish I was there. But the
gap is closing hay?
                                    Love
                                    Tom
