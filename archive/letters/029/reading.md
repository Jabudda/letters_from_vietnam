Dear Dad, Mom & Kids

Well here I am, still down in Cam Rhan Bay. We've been down here since the 31st and haven't seen Australia lately. We finally gave up yesterday and are now awaiting to get back to our unit. Boy, what a let down hay? Oh well, such is life. At least I got plenty of rest down here so really ain't too bad off. We even went swimming down by the ocean almost every day. I'll send some pictures of that to you all. The water sure was warm and salty. This is the cleanist I've been since my last R&R. I really wanted to get out of this country bad but should be happy with what I got hay? We seen a couple guys from our unit down here going on R&R. They left after us by about 4 days. The hill got hit again as we were told. Sure hope ol' Snoopy is OK, & everyone else of course.

The reason I prolonged writing was in hopes of leaving here so I could personally call and wish you all a Happy Easter. But things didn't go as planned - as usual. Today is Easter so a late but Happy Easter to all of you from me who loves and misses you all so much. I would of wrote sooner if I knew this was going to happen. But you know me, always hoping for the best and never giving up. But now I have too. I sure would have loved to hear all your voices again I was really looking forward to that and other things. Oh well, the next time I talk to you I'll be a real live civilian sounds good hay? No sense feeling sorry for myself. Right? Right!! Boy, it sure is hot down here. I'm glad its nice & secure down here cause we (John & I) sure got some nice peaceful sleep. I feel like a new person - almost. I'll be sending this money home the first chance I get. Did you get the last $200 yet? From March's check. Sure hope so.

How's my school early out paper coming. Hope you got that one letter telling what the school would have to write for my early out. If I can get that letter pretty soon, things should be all set. It has to be phrased about in the same words as the copy I sent you. The copy I sent was to give you & them a general idea of how the letter must be. Maybe it's back on the hill waiting for me already - sure hope so. Then we could get the ol' ball rolling and keep our fingers crossed. I could get out between June & Sept. (90 days early) If June, thats the month after next - sure sounds good hay? Oh well, soon we'll find out - or I will.

So how's the weather back home? Really getting nice I bet. If things go right I could be home for summer. Then Petey & I could go for a plane ride. If he's a good boy. Does he still remember about the plane ride. Did you tell him I said he has to be a good boy in order to go. What did he say about that? Boy, I can't wait to get home. Thats all I can think about anymore. Being a civilian and especially being home with my family and friends. One really learns how to appreciate the smallest things in life after being over here. Last year at this time I was home on leave from over here. About the same time as when Brunner Kruse & Wolf got killed. And before that it was Del and many others - especially during Tet of last year. I sure hope & pray they end this mess so others will never have to come here. But thats just wishful thinking hay? Maybe not though. Who knows what tomorrow will bring hay? Well gang, guess I'll close for now and go back on my flight in hopes of getting back to the unit today. So take care of yourselves and God bless all of you from your ol' son & brother who loves & misses you all so darn much. Happy Easter again. Better late then never hay?

Love & miss all of you  
Tom

P.S. Say "hi" to all for me.

P.S. Did Petey find his Easter basket. I can just picture him and all of you now sitting around having the annual Easter dinner. Boy, wish I was there. But the gap is closing hay?

Love  
Tom
