Dear Mom,
    Hi, Surprised you hay with a good
ol' letter to you from ol' me! By the
time you get this Dad will pro-
bably be off to the North woods
getting meat for our starving family
And of course you'll be home
twiddling your thumbs, wondering
what to do next. Right? Oh
well, I doubt if your really angry
cause you know how much Dad
enjoys hunting. And like you said
to me once, "when we're happy
your happy". That goes for Dad
too hay?? So you must be happy.
    Well, by now your "favorite"
season should be in. Think
we'll have a white Xmas??
Sure hope so! Of course you do
too right. I know Petey does.
I can just picture him playing

[page 2, numbered "2" in a circle, over an ink blot]
out in the snow and all of you
on Xmas day. Sure is a nice picture
or thought. At least this is the
last one I'll miss.
    I wonder where Jim is now??
Maybe if you call his house his
Mom would know hay?
    I wonder if ol' Nixon is going
to settle this mess over here. It'll
probably take another few years.
    There's only one main reason
why I regret being over here.
And that is cause I know of
the worrying your going through.
When I came home last time
and seen all your faces - well
I never seen such happy faces
and people in my whole life.
I just wanted to sit down
and cry for joy. But somehow
I held most of it back. Thats

[page 3, also numbered "2" in a circle]
why I asked to see Petey right
away cause I knew if I would
of stood there [possible reading: anyte] longer I
would of broke out crying too.
But those would of been a
years supply of tears of happiness.
I'll bet as soon as I rang the
door bell that day you knew it
was me. That was the greatest
feeling in my life. I just stood
there and looked at the house
at first - not really believing I
was home. But when I seen
you all come to the door I knew
my dreaming was over. I was
so happy.
    And now I'm going through
the same thing again and know
you are too. But at least this
time there will be no going
back to the army - _never_. That

[page 4, numbered "3" in a circle]
is both a nice thought & big relief
for me and know you feel the same.
    It was terribly hard for me
to come back over here. I thought
I did a good job of covering it but
guess not. And then when I read
your letter to me down in Oakland
I felt like a brick wall hit
me. It was really a shock - I
mean not knowing that you
knew all along. I guess one can't
hide nothing from his parents
anymore. I didn't bother to hide
my orders back home cause I
figured you wouldn't be able to
read them. I know Ed saw them
and couldn't make out nothing
so figured you wouldn't either.
Guess I was wrong though.
    I know one couldn't ask for
a better family as I have. Do

[page 5, numbered "4" in a circle]
you ever look at all of us Mom
and get a happy feeling knowing
that you brought us all into
this world. I imagine you do. It
must be a wonderful feeling, one
that only a mother would know.
    Well Mom, guess I talked enough
cause I got to get to work now
Bah! Bah! So I want to tell you
before I go that I know your
the best Mother a guy could ever
have & I thank God that
your mine. So take good care
of yourself and God bless you.
I love & miss you very much
but as we said last year - the
gap is closing. But this time it
will be for good. By for now.
                                  Your ol' son
                                    Tom
