Dear Family,

Hi there. Received your letter today which was dated Feb. 25th. Today is March 4th or 5th. But anyways - all is well.

Ma, I'm sorry I missed your birthday but we've been really busy late. Well anyways here's a "happy birthday" to you. Sorry its late but as the "ol' saying goes", better late then never. Right? I'm sure when I get out we'll make up for it hay?

I'm sure glad to hear that our "neighbor" got back from Korea. Is he out of the army? Sure hope so! I remember that day very vividly. That's when I was getting "short". This time last month I only had a few days left in Viet Nam and how happy I was!! But here we are again. (Snoopy just walked in) But anyways - someday "soon?" I'll be home - for good that is. I can't wait! Like you said though - we must be patient. It really is hard to do though.

I really was surprised [possible reading: Jackie] [possible reading: Was] called to get my address. Here I thought she was married already. Yes, I remember the guy. It was a long time ago. Remember when I went to summer school for driving and math (I think) That's when I knew him. Her mother just better not be playing "cupid" or anything. Cause I sure don't intend to be married for a long time. I got so many things in life I want to do before I get "hitched". I got to darn close to getting married once already. Sure am glad I didn't. At times I guess I'm just to easy of a going guy, ... but I'm learning. At times it always seems like I learn the hard way though. Such is life hay?

I hope Pat gets that job. Harley Davidson is a good company to work for - if you get a real good job. So much on Pat.

Yes, I too, wish they would start bombing the north as you said. But who are we - hay? At times I feel like I'm just a machine. So many things are "easier said then done" though. - So I didn't know that the NVA didn't draft Catholics. I don't know where you read that. They draft anybody they can get, - men, women and children. Actually there isn't much of a draft for them. Its forced upon them. I and many others would do the same if they gave me a choice of my family or me. You see what I mean. That's the kind of mess these people are in - one of the many kinds.

So you going to get a carpet hay. What color is it? Just as long as it isn't (that OD (army green) I'll be happy. I just can't wait to walk up to be home again. Mainly to see all of you - my family whom I am so proud of.

I sure hope Chuckie makes out OK! I just don't know if that guy will ever learn. Someday he will - the hard way. I just hope it doesn't cost him or others their lives. But the way he's going I don't really know.

Well, guess I'll close for now and get some sleep. I had guard last nigh and didn't get any sleep and will have to make up for it tonight. Cause I'll have the "honors" again tomorrow. So until then gang, take good care of yourselves and God bless all of you from me who loves and misses all of you so much.

Your son & brother  
Tom

P.S. Say "hi" to all - thanks
