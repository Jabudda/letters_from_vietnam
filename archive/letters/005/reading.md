Dear Dad, Mom + Kids,

Hi there! Today is June 19th or 20th and all is well. Just switched pens. As you can probably tell. Well, lets see - where did I leave off. Oh yeah. We're up here in Dak To right now. Got here yesterday by chopper. If you watch the CBS news you may get lucky and see me. Cause they was on our hill and the Special Forces one right next to us taking newsreels. Right when we were getting on the chopper he was taking pictures of us. Wow! Finally made the movies. If you look real hard you might recognize me. But in a way we all looked the same. All full of mud. I'm the only one in the bunch with sunglasses though. Just like a Hollywood actor hay! HA!! Don't mind the looks of the hill we're on cause we all know it looks like a junk yard. Thats exactly what it looks like from the Special Forces hill or from the air. What a mess. I guess their going to name our hill Fire Base Hawkins cause he was the *first* to die when we took the hill. It sure is a shame. But what can one do. Many more have followed him since we been there. Things are starting to get better and the NVA are finding out. Right now the only infantry we have in the area are the ARVN's and also (get this) *Cambodians*. [crossed out: The Cambodian] Cambodia doesn't want the NVA on their border so are helping us. Those Cambodians are some real good fighters and great people. I never thought people could be so friendly. One Cam. gave me an extra gas mask he had cause mine was ruined as we been getting hit with gas lately. Don't worry, its just tear gas. Its not harmful but sure the heck irritating! I have to admit that I was happy to leave Ben Het but in a few days will probably go back which also I'm glad of. I left alot back there. Not so much material things but I guess its too hard to explain. But know you know what I mean. Come two months at the most I believe that I have did my part. Maybe not as the army has wanted cause I never was a real straight soldier, meeting his standards and all. But when it come to helping out such as these people here and any body that needed help, I tried and did my best. No, I'm not bragging cause thats about the only good thing I've done since I been here. When it comes to military courtesy, personal appearance and things of that nature I'm considered a dud in the army's eyes. I could just never see some of the "chicken shit" things they want a person to do. Dad knows what I mean cause he was in the same "boat" many years ago.

Boy, it sure will be great to be home again - for good that is. What a wonderful day that will be. It still feels like only a dream but this is one dream that will come true hay? Can just picture Petey running around the yard and playing and all of us together. It sure will be a nice time of the year too won't it? Pretty soon all of you will be on vacation and then we can say next month I'll be home and we'll be together again I sure am a lucky guy to have the wonderful family I do have. Not many people are as fortunate as I am.

Well guess 'tis time to close and get some sleep, seeing how it's getting dark out and we have no lights here. And so closes another day (a whole 24 hrs!) with about 1,440 hours to go until THE DAY. So take good care of yourselves God bless all of you, and in the near future we'll all be together again, one big happy family! Bye Bye for now.

Love + miss you all much  
Tom

P.S. Say "hi" to all for me. Thanks.

Tom
