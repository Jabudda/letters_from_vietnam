Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,
    Hi there - Today is May 5th I
think and all is well. Not
much is new lately. John
is in the hospital. He went last
night. They think it [underlined: may] be
malaria and his temp. went
from 101° to 105°. I sure hope he
is all right. Will probably know
for sure what he has in the
next couple of days.
    So the guys quit at my ol'
turkey farm, hay. Guess they couldn't
handle the hard work HA. Beat
cutting grass at a cemetary!!! Boy
what next. - probably be grave
robbers or something hay? They
should get a good job I agree.
Especially Pat seeing how his
military obligation is over with.
Oh well - everyone to their
own opinion hay? I wonder

[page 2, numbered "2" in a circle]

    Boy - did I get a big box

ington High School. I'll send
you the letter they sent also. There
must have been a 100 books in
it! I wonder how they got my
name. I'll have to send them
a thank you letter. It sure
was a big surprise to me!
    I have to go to work again
in a few minutes. I also got
a letter from Dad and [possible reading: Mick]
the other day. Sure was glad
to hear from [underlined: you all]. (southern
talk!)
    Well, the gap is closing
yet hay. At times I sure
find it hard to believe

out. On that school out

bless all of you from your ol'
tired son & brother who loves
and misses all of you more
then a letter or words could
ever say. Bye for now.
                              Love & miss
                                  Tom
