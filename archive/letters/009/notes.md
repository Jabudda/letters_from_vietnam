A P.S. to Mom follows and runs onto page 4, where he signs again: "Your son, Tom". No envelope was scanned for this letter.

## Open questions for the family

1. **The year.** "Today is May 5th I think", with no year. He writes "the gap is closing", "I am finally getting out" and of a "school out". Letter 008 (dated "May 10", year uncertain) also talks of getting out early for "M.T.C.". The two letters look like the same spring (interpretation). Is it 1969?
2. **"School out."** "They say you get out about 7 days before your date of school." Letter 008 hoped for "almost... a month drop". Does the family know when he got out and whether he started school (Milwaukee Technical College?) that year?
3. **John in the hospital.** "They think it may be malaria and his temp. went from 101° to 105°." Who was John (a buddy in his unit?), and did he recover?
4. **"My ol' turkey farm."** He worked at a turkey farm before the Army, and "the guys quit" there and went to "cutting grass at a cemetary". Does the family know the farm and who "the guys" were? Is "Pat" (whose "military obligation is over with") one of them?
5. **Names to check:** "when (or if) Glen will go in and also Tim" (the first name is unclear; it could be "Glen" or "Dan"); "Did Ernie get drafted yet?"; "a letter from Dad and Mick" (Mick is his brother Michael, per Andy). Who are the others, and may their names be published?
6. **The box of books from Washington High School.** "There must have been a 100 books in it! I wonder how they got my name." Was Washington High School his own school (Milwaukee or elsewhere)? Does the letter they sent survive?
7. ~~**The Harley.**~~ Answered: Dad never bought the Harley (Andy, 2026-11-10).
8. ~~**The mark under the second signature.**~~ Answered: a decorative accent under his name (Andy, 2026-11-10).

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** He is working ("I have to go to work again in a few minutes"), expects to be "finally getting out", and has heard that under a school early-out "you get out about 7 days before your date of school".
- **Historical context:** In the Vietnam era, Defense Department rules allowed enlisted soldiers to be released early to enroll in college or a vocational/technical school (DoD Directive 1332.15, issued 1959 and reissued 5 January 1968). *Source:* [DoD Directive 1332.15 history (GlobalSpec)](https://standards.globalspec.com/std/131867/dod-d-1332-15). See also Letter 008 notes.
- **Historical context:** Harley-Davidson's 74-cubic-inch big twin, sold as the "1200", powered the FL Electra Glide. From 1966 it used the new "Shovelhead" engine of about 1,208 cc. *Sources:* [RM Sotheby's, 1973 Harley-Davidson FLH 1200 Electra Glide (lot history)](https://rmsothebys.com/auctions/hf23/lots/r0091-1973-harleydavidson-flh-1200-electra-glide); [Harley-Davidson Shovelhead engine (Wikipedia mirror)](https://bafybeiemxf5abjwjbikoz4mc3a3dla6ual3jsgpdr4cjr3oz3evfyavhwq.ipfs.dweb.link/wiki/Shovelhead_engine.html).
- **Interpretation:** "A Harley 1200 c.c." very likely means one of these 74-cubic-inch big twins. The model is unknown.
- **Unknown:** Where he was when he wrote this; the letter names no place or unit.
- **Cross-reference (from later letters):** The Harley story runs on. On 13 May 1969 Dad had given a friend $180 for "a '52, Harley 1200cc." from the friend's brother, to come to Milwaukee by freight train (Letter 039); by about 21 June he had "cancelled the deal on that cycle" (Letter 025); and on 19 July he asked about the $180 check and reminded the family of "our deal… using the money for a good ol' dog instead of a cycle" (Letter 041).
