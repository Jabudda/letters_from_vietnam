Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,
    Hi there, I received your Easter
Letter today and sure was glad
to hear from you. Today is April
11th, [circled:] 1969 (of course), and all is well.
    I'm pretty sure I did mention
I did get your Easter package
by now. And if not, once again
thanks an awful lot.
    I'm sorry you all didn't get a
phone call like you (and I) hoped
it would be. All I can say
is that the next time you
hear from me I'll be a civilian.
A real live one too!!! No if, [possible reading: ands]
or buts!! I sure did want (and
plan) to call all of you on Easter
Sunday but no such luck! It
would have been wonderful
though. Boy, I long to hear
all your voices again but
know that isn't possible for

[page 2, not numbered]
awhile, anyways. It sure sounds
like you had a good Easter. Oh
well, next year I'll be there for
Easter. Really I'll be home this
year but next Easter is what
I mean. Must clarify myself
ya know.
    Well how did my ol' cousins
do in Fla. Had a good time I
hope. I also hope to be hear-
ing from them soon. Its
too bad Steve couldn't
go. Why couldn't he. Probably
his parents I suppose! Such is
life hay.
    I bet its really getting nice back
home - weather wise. Pretty soon
the grass will be around
- along with the weeds of
course. Can't have one with
out the other ya know.
    Well dear family, I guess I'll

[page 3, not numbered]
close for now and get some
sleep. So take care of yourselves
and God bless all of you
from your son & brother who
loves and misses you all is more
then words or a letter can
ever say. By for now
                                  Love & miss you all
                                    Tom
                                    [a decorative flourish below the signature]
