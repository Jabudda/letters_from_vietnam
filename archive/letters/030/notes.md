# Letter 030: notes

## Source

Two sheets of thin lined paper written in black ink, three pages (the third on the back or a separate half-sheet). The pages are not numbered; each runs on from the one before ("isn't possible for / awhile"; "I guess I'll / close for now"). Addressed "Dear Dad, Mom & Kids" and dated in the text **"Today is April 11th, 1969 (of course)"**, with the year circled by Dad himself. Signed "Love & miss you all, Tom", with a decorative flourish, and a P.S. No envelope was scanned.

The scans are numbered 0157, 0158 and 0161; scans 0159 and 0160 belong to the next folder (Letter 031).

## Open questions for the family

1. **The date (written in full).** "Today is April 11th, 1969 (of course)." This is one of only a few letters where Dad wrote the year himself. It comes five days after Letter 029 (Easter, 6 April 1969, Cam Ranh Bay) and three days after Letter 028 (8 April, "back to the hill").
2. **The Easter phone call.** "I'm sorry you all didn't get a phone call like you (and I) hoped… I sure did want (and plan) to call all of you on Easter Sunday but no such luck!" This matches Letter 029, written at Cam Ranh Bay on Easter Sunday: he had hoped to fly out on R&R and phone home. Did the family know he was trying to call?
3. **The cousins in Florida, and Steve.** "How did my ol' cousins do in Fla… Its too bad Steve couldn't go. Why couldn't he. Probably his parents I suppose!" Who were the cousins? Is this Steve the "Stevie" of Letter 019, whose parents Dad also criticised ("I like Steve & Kathleen alot but thank the good Lord I'm not a son of theirs")? If so, Stevie may have been a cousin (interpretation; please confirm).
4. **The Easter package.** What was in it?
