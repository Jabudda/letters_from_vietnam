# Letter 006: notes

## Source

Four pages of lined notebook paper written in black ink, scanned one side each; no backs were scanned. Pages 2 and 3 carry a number in a circle at the top; page 4 is not numbered. A fold across page 1 hides most of one line. The envelope is printed "AFTER 5 DAYS RETURN TO" and addressed in his hand.

The envelope front is addressed to the Jafuta Family in West Allis, Wisconsin, with "Free" where a stamp would go and his return address "T. Jafuta, HHB 3/6 ARTY, APO San FRAN, ZIP CODE 96318". There is no postmark or date on it. The back carries, in blue ink and a different pen from the letter, the military address of **Sgt J. A. Theilke**, H.H.C. 3 Bde, 101st ABN Div., APO 96383, with his service number. Who wrote it on the back is not known. The envelope is kept with this letter in the family's folder; that it is this letter's envelope is assumed, not proven.

## Open questions for the family

1. **The date.** The letter is not dated and the envelope has no postmark. Clues: "About 45 more guards left for me over here - and in the whole army", "The time is nearing an end", and Letter 005 (19 or 20 June 1969) counting 60 days to go. If he pulled guard about once a day, this letter would be from **about early July 1969** (interpretation). Does the family know more?
2. **The hidden line.** On page 1, a fold in the paper hides most of the line after "The one with the bubble gum and". Could the page be rescanned with the crease gently flattened? That might recover it.
3. **Readings to check:** "for Snoopy" (his dog, Letter 002; the word looks like "Snoppy" or "Snoopy"); "ol' Dott" (the same name as the Steve [Dott] of Letter 003?); "I'd like for to believe"; "I ain't very happy"; "Bye, by for now".
4. **Jim.** He asks three times for Jim's address and hasn't heard from him "for a few months now". The back of the envelope carries Sgt J. A. Theilke's address with the 101st Airborne Division. Is this Jim Theilke (Letters 002–004)? Does anyone know who wrote the address on the back, and when? May his unit be published? His service number is withheld either way.
5. **Petey's letter.** "Real glad to hear Petey liked my letter to him." The family's folder has a letter marked "Letter 18_Petey"; it may be that letter (to check when we reach it).
6. **"Blue-printing in one of my courses."** Letter 003 talks of switching his college plan to a plumbing apprenticeship, and Andy says he became a plumber. Does the family know which course this was?
7. **Privacy.** Page 3 tells, with his own humor, of an accident caused by a week of diarrhea. It is his story in his words; the family decides whether it is published.

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** He writes "before I get ready for guard" and "About 45 more guards left for me over here - and in the whole army. That's still alot hay."
- **Interpretation:** "45 more guards" reads as a countdown of nights on guard duty before he left Vietnam and, as he saw it, the army. With Letter 005's "about 1,440 hours" (60 days) on 19 or 20 June 1969, this letter fits early July 1969. This is an estimate, not a date.
- **Verified fact (from the letter):** "whenever something happens I'd much rather be up then sleeping. It also gives a guy a lot of time to think about everything... Also 'talk' to God alot too. He sure is a good ol' listener hay."
- **Verified fact (from the letter):** "As you probably know ol' [Dott] is still around, I sure was glad to see him too." In Letter 003 (11–12 May 1969) he worried that a Steve [possible reading: Dott] "may be worse off" and that "Steve's unit didn't know where he was." **Interpretation:** if this is the same man, Dad's worry in May was answered by July. The reading of the name is not certain in either letter.
- **Verified fact (envelope back):** Sgt J. A. Theilke, H.H.C. 3 Bde, 101st ABN Div. **Historical context:** the 101st Airborne Division served in Vietnam for nearly seven years and fought in 45 operations. *Source:* [screamingeagle.org, "Division History"](https://screamingeagle.org/?p=146). **Unknown:** where Sgt Theilke was in 1969; nothing here places him at any battle.
- **Verified fact (from the letter):** "That sure sounds like a good idea about getting blue-printing in one of my courses." **Interpretation:** a blueprint course fits the plan in Letter 003 to switch to a plumbing apprenticeship; he did become a plumber (Andy, Letter 003 notes).
