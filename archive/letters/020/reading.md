Viet Nam

Hi gang,

Today is Oct. 29 and I got your letter today - dated Oct. 23. I also got the plumbing book & food the other day ... finally I kinda expect to see ol' [possible reading: Jim] coming here in the next couple of days. If he comes here, he should be here by the 31st or 1st. I'm So Petey is taking guitar & singing lessons now hay. Maybe he'll be Elvis Presely's replacement when he gets to old hay. I can see Petey now - a movie star?? Well maybe a comedian.

Hope Dad's back is better by now. Better stop lifting all that heavy stuff Dad. I mean it too. We got alot of years of hunting to do, You, Mike, *Petey* & I. Right? Right! Next year will be a good year for me. Glad to hear you got a pheasant this year. I shot a big ol' bird the other day and gave it to a villager. Don't know what kind it was, hope it was in season HA! It looked something like a vulture, not to tasty. Food is food to some of these people though. Maybe soon things will be straightened out over here, I hope to God so. I never want to have to see my brothers come over here or to some other war. Just the thought of it makes me feel sick. That's why I feel guilty about me here, knowing what you are going through. I'm sorry but its for a cause which I believe in. Things always work out for the best hay?

So ol' Mike is in J.A. Glad to hear it. How many girlfriends you make there Mike? I remember when Pat & I were in it. It was fun.

No, Sharyn won't have her Baby until December. I don't know if I told you but her husband is over here somewhere. Yeah, Karen did have a small accident. Some gal hit her. Thats about all I know of it for now.

Boy, that picture of me standing with Karen was really funny. Looks like I was sleeping. The other one decides to look too bad. The one of Petey taking his "siesta" sure was cute. I'll bet that was one of the better naps he took hay. When I finish this plumbing book I have I'll tell you and sure would appreciate it if you would send Lee's. I also need some *references* to *write* to - HINT.

So Pat's getting out hay? I sure hope all works out for the best for him. To tell you the truth, I'd like to write to him, but don't it weather to write or print big letters. Also [possible reading: Tim is in] OK? Is he still going with [possible reading: Beverly]? Hope so, cause I knew he really loved her.

Well now that I got Davey's address I'll write him. Hope all is working out for the best for him. Someday in the near future we'll both be out. Happy Day!!

Well dear family I guess I'll close for now and get some sleep, sure can use it. I'm the proudest guy around to have a family such as I have. Every day things are looking up for me - cause I'm a day closer to getting home for good. I'll feel good too, knowing that I fulfilled my military obligation in the best way I could. Sure at times I'm scared over here and feel like crying, but I guess that's natural - as you said. Well I guess I'll say good by for now and get some sleep. God bless all of you from your ol' son + brother who misses all of you very much. By for now

Love all of you  
Tommy

P.S. I lost Eleanor's address (your mom's & sister's) Send them OK? Thanks
