Dear Dad,
    Hi there. Today is Aug [6, circled] and all is
pretty good. Got your letter today
and sure was glad to hear from you.
Especially the part where you said
you know what. About the pup
of course. I sure was happy and still
am of course. Can't wait to see him
but mainly all of my family naturally.
I bet you built a run way kennel
the length of our garage hay? If you
wait a few more days I'll be home and
we could even put a cement floor in
hay Dad? Hell, we can go all out and build
a good one. Better have paid half out of
my "fat" [inserted above: ??] bank roll. It means alot
to me Dad for reasons that are hard
to explain but easy to see. I guess mainly
cause then we can really call it our dog
and take the same pride & care in him.
What's going to be his name Dad??
It sure will be fun to raise him & train

[page 2, numbered "2" in a circle]
him. Like when we had Fritz. But time
and experience have changed me for the
better. Remember how Fritz used to fall
over his own feet when he was a puppy?
He sure turned out a great dog though.
This one will be good too - just know
it. Can't wait to get home Dad.
    It has been very quiet (too quiet) lately
but things are starting to pick up again.
In a way Im glad. Cause that just
knowing something is too happen and
waiting is really nerve wracking. Thats
why when ever we get hit I feel "relieved"
in a certain sense. Sounds crazy hay Dad?
But I think you know what I mean.
I'll be so happy and relieved to
leave this country behind me. The days
sure are dragging [inserted above: by] [crossed out: illegible] but 24 hrs. is 24 hrs.
hay. The rains are starting to break
up a bit. Happy about that. Being
wet isn't too bad but the mud gets
to me after awhile. Oh well, its almost
over with.

[page 3, scan 0069; numbered "3" in a circle, written over the word "believe"]
It sure is hard to believe though. When
I hear your voice on the phone and know
I'll be home in a few hours - I guess I'll
be one of the happiest [crossed out: people] persons in
the world. That day still isn't here yet
and seems like it never will - but it will
won't it Dad?
    Well, just ate supper. Thats one thing
how I'll never get over is how
they (army) cooks such lousy food.
I can see over here but even in the
states its lousy. Oh well, not
many more meals left.
    So how's business been lately.
Good I hope. I'll be happy when
I start my apprent. some day.
    About in 4 more days I'll stop
writing. Cause 7 days from then I
should be home if every thing goes
off as planned. But you know how
the army is.

[page 4, scan 0068; numbered "4" in a circle]
Well Dad, guess I'll go out and

changed much since you been in
hay Dad?
    It sure will be good to be home

believe but its true. I just can't
wait to see all of you again and
for good. I know day dreaming
and thinking about home isn't too
healthy of a thing to do over here.
But its still about all I do
even while working. I just can't
