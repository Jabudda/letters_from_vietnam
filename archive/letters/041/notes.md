# Letter 041: notes

## Source

Two sheets of printed letter paper, pale blue with a map of Vietnam at the lower left (the same design as the "United States Armed Forces - Vietnam" envelope), written in black ink on one side, four pages. The second page is numbered in a circle (possible reading: 2) and the third has a small number at the top (possible reading: 3); the text runs straight on from page to page ("Such is life hay. / Boy, I just found a news paper"; "are ya upset? / I feel like just quitting"; "home soon - but not soon / enough!"), so nothing appears to be missing. Addressed "Dear Dad, Mom & Kids" and dated in the text "today is the 19th". Signed "Your son & ol' brother, Tom".

The printed "UNITED STATES ARMED FORCES - VIETNAM" envelope is addressed in his hand to the "Jafuta Family" in West Allis, Wisconsin, with "Free" and the return address "T. Jafuta, HHB 3/6 ARTY, APO SAN FRAN 96318". There is no postmark. **"7/19" is written in blue ink in another hand**, like the dates on the envelopes of Letters 010, 012, 023, 025 and 034. Only the front was scanned.

## Open questions for the family

1. **The date.** "today is the 19th"; "7/19" on the envelope; he has "just found a news paper telling about the moon flight… They say its going to be on TV too". Apollo 11 launched on 16 July 1969 and landed on 20 July. So this is **19 July 1969** (interpretation, a strong one).
2. **Phan Thiet (a cross-reference).** "I left Phan Thiet yesterday." Letter 023 (16 July 1969) has him at Phan Thiet with John after being "kicked out of Saigon"; this letter has him at the airport at Cam Ranh on his way "back to my unit" in the highlands.
3. **"When we were in Ben Het most of the things in my wallet were destroyed."** His own words about Ben Het, after the fact. Compare Letter 034 (18 June 1969, "We're still up in Ben Het yet") and Andy's family accounts in Letter 034's notes.
4. **The $180 and "our deal" (cross-references).** In Letter 039 (13 May) he borrowed $180 from John for a '52 Harley and asked for a $180 money order; in Letter 025 (about 21 June) he cancelled the cycle; here the check is "back at my unit" and he reminds the family of "our deal… using the money for a good ol' dog instead of a cycle". **"If its a male - we ought to name it 'Fritz.'"** Andy has said Grandpa had about three dogs named Fritz. Was the dog bought in 1969 named Fritz?
5. **The last cigarette.** "I know this is going to upset you - especially you Mom… I just smoked my last cigarette and they don't sell any here." Did he quit when he got home? **Answered (Andy, 2026-11-10):** "Dad never quit smoking."
6. **"At the end of the month another big offensive is suppose to happen."** What he had heard; nothing here says what happened.
7. **Readings to check:** the page numbers on pages 2 and 3; "Cam. Rhan".

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** On 19 July (1969, interpretation) he was at the airport at Cam Ranh, having left Phan Thiet the day before and slept on a bench, waiting for a flight back to his unit "up in the high lands".
- **Historical context:** Apollo 11 was launched on 16 July 1969 and landed on the Moon on 20 July 1969; its flight was televised. *Sources:* [Britannica, "Apollo 11"](https://www.britannica.com/explore/space/apollo-11/); [NASA, "Triumph After Splashdown of Apollo 11"](https://www.nasa.gov/?p=416229). Compare Letter 038 (25 July), "I hear that they landed on the moon safe and also got back to earth."
- **Historical context:** Cam Ranh was a U.S. air base and seaport on the coast. *Source:* [Wikipedia, "Cam Ranh Base"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cam_Ranh_Base). (See also Letters 028, 029 and 031, also written at or near Cam Ranh Bay.)
