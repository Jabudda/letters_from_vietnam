Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,

Hi there! Well today is the 19th and I'm in the air port in Cam. Rhan awaiting to head back to my unit. Sooo thought I'd make use of my spare time by writing a letter home to the best family around who I'll be seeing in the near future.

I left Phan Thiet yesterday and spent the night here sleeping on a bench - just like a bum. My poor body aches all over. Must be getting old or something hay.

Boy, it sure is hot out down here. At least up in the high lands its alot cooler. Thats the only thing better about it in my books. Its been nice and quiet up there for a few days. But at the end of the month another big offensive is suppose to happen. I sure hope thats the last one I ever see. Oh well, I'll be home soon anyways - won't I. Thats about all that counts.

Boy, got a long wait. Just found out my flight leaves in 5 hrs. if it leaves at all. Don't really care much cause I'm in no hurry to get back. Although it is a heck of alot cooler up there. Such is life hay.

Boy, I just found a news paper telling about the moon flight. It sure is wild hay. They say its going to be on TV too. Maybe when I get home they'll show a re-run or something. I sure would love to see it. That kind of stuff really interests me alot. Mainly cause I find it so hard to believe.

No kidding, All I can think of anymore is going home and getting out of this country. Its going to be a really wonderful feeling seeing all of you again - for good. I just never really knew how good I had it until I came in the army. A lesson learned and one that I'll never, never forget or couldn't even if I wanted too.

Boy, I bet the weather back home is really nice hay. Hope it stays good so I can go swimming a couple of times when I'm *out* ← sounds good hay?

I know this is going to upset you - especially you Mom. But I decided to tell all of you. I just smoked my *last* cigarette and they don't sell any here. Well, are ya upset? I feel like just quitting altogether but easier said then done. Especially over here. I get too nervous at times. When I get home I'll give it a try though. Its a stupid habit to get into and a much harder habit to quit. Live & learn though.

Oh yeah, remember that $180 check you sent to me. Well its back at my unit and I don't need it. So what should I do. Its pretty well messed up cause when we were in Ben Het most of the things in my wallet were destroyed. So should I send it home still or can you cancel it without the check. I meant to ask you that before but always forgot. And also remember our deal??? Don't say you forgot now. About using the money for a good ol' dog instead of a cycle. Remember??? Well whats the story?? If its a male - we ought to name it "Fritz."

Well dear family, guess I'll close for now and see what's the story on my plane. So until we write again - take care and God bless all of you. Be seeing you in the near future. There's just no words to describe how happy I'll be. Say "hi" to all back home and tell em I'll be home soon - but not soon enough! By by for now.

Your son & ol' brother,
Tom
