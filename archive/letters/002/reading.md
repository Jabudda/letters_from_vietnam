Dear Dad, Mom, + Kids,

Hi, today is the 22nd (I think) and all is well. I'v been out here at the Oasis for the last 3 or 4 days and didn't get much time to write so will catch up on it now.

No one knows how long we'll be here cause there's alot of stuff to be done yet. Its unbelievable how dusty it is out here. We all look like red heads (red dust, out here) In places the dust is about ankle deep - no lie. You remember the dust on the movie films last year I think. I don't know which is worst - that or mud. I think mud cause I always slip on it and get full of it. - Clumsy me!

I received your sausage and crackers the other day but didn't get a chance to thank you all so will do so now. Thanks alot - it sure went good with my two beer; Burp!! I hope by now you got the letter telling you I got the cake and how good it was. Also did Kathy ever get the money order I sent. Be sure to tell me O.K. Thanks.

I have Snoopy out here with me. I sure do care for him alot. It sounds dumb but at times when Im alone and lonely I start talking to him. The funny thing is it almost seems as if he can understand me. He cocks his head to one side and just listens or at least looks like it. Oh well, so Im loosing my marbles - just kidding.

Hay! - you better start keeping Petey away from Danny. I sure as heck don't want a "strange" brother. I mean it too. Thats how stuff like that probably starts!! Right? Right!!

I should get a haircut one of these days. I haven't gotten one for about 3½ weeks or so. Its not that Im against long hair but this dust just sticks to it. It feels like hair spray caked all over. It even gets sticky when you (I) sweat. A real mess.

Yawn, I'm tired as usual *but* letter writing must go on while I got the *oppertunity* [written above in capitals: BIG WORD] Right?

I also got the pictures of when I was home today. They turned out pretty good hay? The only one missing it then was guess who - you Mom! Well, what kind of explanation do you have for that? - Thats no excuse. Some one else could of held the camera like Pat or somebody else. I won't accept no excuses. I'll have to put them in my album tonight. I sure do like to look at that thing when I get lonely or depressed. Its quite alot looked at.

So youre going to put an order in for a bed pretty soon hay. It sure sounds good to me, - real good. Im not fussy as to how hard or how soft it is. After being over here so long I can sleep on anything. If I could count every place I ever slept as a "bed" I would probably have had the most "beds" in the world. The worst is sleeping on muddy sand bags or a muddy hole. That I hate! Dust is bad too cause when the guns go off dust blows all over you (I mean - me) Oh well, it'll all come to an end. Right? A very happy one at that!
