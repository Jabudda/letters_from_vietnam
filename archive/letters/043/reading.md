Dear Dad, Mom & Kids [possible reading]

Like "Hi"!

Today is Thanksgiving Day . . . & I have alot to be thankful for!! Of course it was a lonely one - but thank God this will be my last one in the army!

When I think of all of you "celebrating" I kinda get to feeling sick. I'm lonely - yes - but I'm also thankful - for having the wonderful family as I do have. I just wish I was there to be with all of you. But like I said so many times before - "Our Day will come"! Right? Of course I am. And it will be the happiest one in my life. Just being with all of you and knowing I will never have to be so many miles apart - really makes me happy!!

Next month is good ol' Xmas. I sure hope it is a happy one for all of you. Naturally mine will be a sad one but the last one in wich I will be gone!! But like I said in my last letter . . . I *hope* to have a surprise for all of you around Xmas. If all does work out - this "surprise" will bring me much closer to all of you - even though I won't be home for Xmas.

Well I'm just dying to tell you what that "surprise?" was . . . . . sure! You see - I'm taking my R&R to Hawaii next month on the 13th. And I plan on making it home from there. Of course I'm not suppose to but who will know. It will only be for about 4 days *but* I want to be home for at least *[possible reading: near]* Xmas. Maybe *we* can have an "early" Xmas hay? I hope so. It sure would be wonderful. I was going to surprise all of you but *if* all works out well (which it usually does!) we can have an "early" Xmas hay? It sure will be wonderful as I said before.

Got a pen now. Sure beats using a crummy pencil. But I done run out of things to "talk" about. So . . . if all works out next month - I'll be seeing you all. So until then - take care of yourselves & God bless all of you -

Your son & brother,
Tom
