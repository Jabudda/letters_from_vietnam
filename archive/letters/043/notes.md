## Source

Two sheets torn from a spiral notebook (pink rules, a pink centre line, torn holes along the top), written on both sides, four pages. Pages 1 to 3 are in faint **pencil**; the last line of page 3 and all of page 4 are in **blue ink** ("Got a pen now. Sure beats using a crummy pencil."). Pages 2 and 3 are numbered in circles. ### Where this transcription differs from the earlier draft

The earlier draft paraphrases most sentences. Among the larger differences: it has "thanks to all this, well… in my heart" where the page reads "thank God this will be my last one in the army!"; "your pal in Hawaii might move on the 13th" where the page reads "I'm taking my R&R to Hawaii next month on the 13th"; and it leaves out "Of course I'm not suppose to but who will know."

## Open questions for the family

1. **The date.** "Today is Thanksgiving Day… thank God this will be my last one in the army!" and an R&R to Hawaii "next month on the 13th". In Letter 031 (23 December 1968) Dad had just been home "a just 4 days" in mid-December 1968. So this is very likely **Thanksgiving, 28 November 1968**, early in his second tour (interpretation, a strong one). Letter 022 (postmarked 20 November 1968) says this Christmas is "the last one I'll miss".
2. **The surprise (an answer to Letter 031).** "I'm taking my R&R to Hawaii next month on the 13th. And I plan on making it home from there. Of course I'm not suppose to but who will know. It will only be for about 4 days." This is the plan behind the four days at home in December 1968 that Letter 031 describes. Letter 017 recalls landing in Hawaii "both times" and calling home. Does the family remember Dad turning up in December 1968? Was it a surprise?
3. **Privacy.** The line "Of course I'm not suppose to but who will know" is Dad's own account of going home from R&R. It is transcribed exactly; whether and how it appears in the book and on the site is for the family to decide. Andy has shared a family memory of how the visit ended; it is kept in the private family notes until the family decides. **Decided (Andy, 2026-11-10): published as written** ("Publish everything, no censorship."; "Please publish the remaining letters.").
4. **"Like I said in my last letter."** Which letter told of the surprise first? (Letter 022, 20 November 1968, is the nearest letter so far.)
5. **Readings to check:** the first line (partly hidden); "[possible reading: near]" (page 3); the word crossed out on page 2; who wrote the small "sp" marks.

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** Written on Thanksgiving Day (1968, interpretation) in pencil until he "got a pen". He planned R&R in Hawaii from the 13th of the next month and expected to be home for "about 4 days".
- **Historical context:** Thanksgiving Day fell on Thursday 28 November in 1968 (the fourth Thursday of November). (Calendar calculation.)
- **Historical context:** Soldiers in Vietnam could take one week of R&R ("rest and recuperation") during a tour, at places including Hawaii, where many met their families. *Source:* see Letter 017 notes. Nothing here has been checked against his records.
