Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,
  Hi there - today is May 17th and
all is well - of course. Right now
we're still out at the Oasis and
may leave tomorrow. I had guard
last night, and saw Steve [possible reading: Dott]
again when all of us went out on
a dumb morning patrol they have
out here. Steve is really a gung-ho
guy or something. I was watching
him the way he acted while we
were out there on patrol. I was
like that my first time here for a
few months but finally got to think-
ing. I went over to [crossed out: unclear] see Steve
tonight but he wasn't around so
decided to write this letter earlier
then planned.
  We sure got soaked last night
on guard but it stopped raining a-
bout 1 or 2 this morning and we
dried off a bit. Thats one good thing
about these clothes we have here.
They're so light weight that
they dry fast.
  Boy, there sure are alot of stars
out tonight. I really get homesick
more then ever on these kind of
nights. Alot of new guys ask me
if I get homesick yet after being
here so long and I tell them
the truth - I always do. Thats one

  Boy, I sure am tired but don't
feel like going to sleep for awhile.
I just don't know what to do
with myself at times. Sometimes it
gets so boring and nerve wracking
that I wish something would hap-
pen just to get it over with. And
then when it does I'm never happy

gether again and it sure will be

or something. And not someone real
