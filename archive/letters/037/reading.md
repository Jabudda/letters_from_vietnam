Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,

Hi there - today is May 17th and all is well - of course. Right now we're still out at the Oasis and may leave tomorrow. I had guard last night, and saw Steve [possible reading: Dott] again when all of us went out on a dumb morning patrol they have out here. Steve is really a gung-ho guy or something. I was watching him the way he acted while we were out there on patrol. I was like that my first time here for a few months but finally got to thinking. I went over to see Steve tonight but he wasn't around so decided to write this letter earlier then planned.

We sure got soaked last night on guard but it stopped raining about 1 or 2 this morning and we dried off a bit. Thats one good thing about these clothes we have here. They're so light weight that they dry fast.

Boy, there sure are alot of stars out tonight. I really get homesick more then ever on these kind of nights. Alot of new guys ask me if I get homesick yet after being here so long and I tell them the truth - I always do. Thats one thing that a guy will never get over if he comes from such a wonderful family as I do. But all I can say for that is the gap is closing - slowly but surely - isn't it??

Boy, I sure am tired but don't feel like going to sleep for awhile. I just don't know what to do with myself at times. Sometimes it gets so boring and nerve wracking that I wish something would happen just to get it over with. And then when it does I'm never happy about it. Someday we'll all be together again and it sure will be wonderful, to say the least. But up until then, patience and prayer will have to do, right?

I heard someone talking that the Church has rejected alot of "saints" such as St. Cristopher and others. Is that true. If it is I sure will have my doubts about things in life (maybe even our religion) If you heard anything about it would you tell me. I sure am curious about it. Many times I prayed to St. Cris. And if he isn't a saint - it would be like me praying to one of my every day friends or something. And not someone real special. You know what I mean?? Maybe its all rumors anyways - hopefully.

I bet it sure is nice out back home. Isn't it?? Pretty soon picnic's hay. Nothing like good ol' summer time or anytime back home as far as that goes.

I bet Petey is really enjoying himself too, hay? By the time I get home he (all of you!!) should have a good tan and be in real good health. Well I'll even get some fishing in too! Nothing like a good ol' fish fry hay, Mom? Or better yet - pheasant or rabbit. If I stay as tired as now I'll be a Rip Van Winkle when I get home and sleep right through everything. It'll be so nice and peaceful though even with Petey running around the house having fun. Can picture it now, my family, good food, and that big soft clean bed.

Well gang, guess I'll close for now and get some sleep. Soooo take good care of yourselves, God bless all of you from me - who loves and misses all of you is more then words can ever say. Take care & say "hi" to all. Thanks.

Your son & brother,
Tom
