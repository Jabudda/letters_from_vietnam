Dear Family,

Well, here I am in Cam Ran Bay awaiting a flight back to my unit. As usual things worked out good for me. Even if they didn't I think it would have been worth it just seeing all of you again. It sure was a just 4 days but I really enjoyed it alot. The worst part about it was leaving of course. Oh well, gotta accept the bad with the good hay. I just can't wait for the day when I'll be called "Mr. Tom".

I'm kinda anxious to get back to my unit and see what happened while I was away and of course to see Snoopy.

*Dec. 23rd*

Hi again

Well - its almost Christmas Eve and all is well. Snoopy sure was happy to see me. He just cried and jumped all over me. He lost alot of weight though. I kinda expected that. I hope in a few more weeks he'll be back in shape.

Not much has happened lately. Had guard last nite and Pleiku Airbase (about 1/2 mi) from us) got the hell knocked out of them. They were built up for it though and suffered few casualties. If they have that Xmas truce this year - they're crazy. They probably think its a good step towards peace but they are 100% wrong. Well so much for that.

I got your Xmas presents yesterday and thanks alot. I really needed the under ware. As a matter of fact - I got a pair on now! Almost makes me feel like a civilian - HA!!

I also got a letter from Davey. He should be home by now. I know he's as happy as can be. And I don't blame him. I'm just glad the next time I will be home will be for good!! I guess no one knows how happy I'll be - except for me & maybe all of you.

Now, the thoughts of me just being home last week seems like a wonderful dream to me. It seems so unreal but it was for real. Boy, it sure was great. To me - its just too bad that I had to come back over here. When I finally realized that I was *home* it seemed that all this was only a dream but it wasn't. When I get out I really don't know how I'll feel or act at first. I guess Dad had that same feeling. I think my first feeling will be one of [possible reading: keyed] up and nervous. It sure will be strange, being home and knowing I'll never have to come back here or to the army again. But thank God my day will come.

Well, gang, guess I'll close for now and get some sleep. God bless all of you from your son & brother, who loves & misses you all very much.

Love & miss you all  
Tommy

P.S. Say "hi" to all & a late Merry Christmas & Happy New year. This is the year (1969) - my year.

By for now  
Tom
