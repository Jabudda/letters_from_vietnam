March 25, 1969
Pleiku, Viet Nam

Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,

Hi there! Sorry about the delay of letters but as usual we've been busy again lately. Today is March 25th. And you know what that means? Well I'll tell ya. Almost exactly 30 months ago I raised my right hand. And joined the army. A whole 30 months ago. Boy thats a long time. Guess how I'll be "celebrating" it? As usual - on guard. I used to always think I would be so happy to only have 3 to 6 months left (depends on the school drop) But it still feels like a life time to go. Oh well, such is life. In a few more days I hope to be in Australia but that all depends on if I get my money orders in time. I'm suppose to be working now but decided to take some time off to write a letter to my #1 family. With a red pen even?

So ol' [possible reading: Danny] will be coming here pretty soon hay? Sure hope he has a nice safe tour. With a little luck and more prayer things usually work out for the best - hay?

Boy, it sure will feel great to get out of here - and the army all together. Its like getting 2 birds with one stone as the old saying goes! Happy Days! I hope Jim is doing OK? Haven't heard from him in a while. I actually owe him a letter but lost his address. Sooo - if you think of it - could you send it - Thanks alot. I wonder how his R&R was. Plenty of wine, women & song I bet.

How's lil' Petey doing. I'll bet the first thing he says when I get home is about the air plane ride. I sure hope so anyways. We'll really have alot of fun. I don't care for planes but I'm sure Petey will make it well worth while. I can't wait to see that rascal again and all the rest of you of course. This time it will be for good. Thats going to be a funny & great feeling after being away from home so dam long. I doubt if I'll even believe its for good cause I was so used to taking leaves and all. This time it will be a permanent one hay. Boy I can't wait. No lie, thats all I think of anymore. I know I'll feel so happy when I walk out of Ft. Louis as a "Mr. Tom." Think I should come home with my uniform one [possible reading: on] - for the last time? I really don't want to but I'll leave that up to you all. Big decision hay?

So how's the weather back home. Real nice about now I bet. That spring air and breeze always feels and smells so nice. Everything feels like its new all over again. I'm glad I'll never miss another season after this one. I sure will be glad to miss these seasons they have over here, and everything along with them. Right? Right!!

How's Petey's girl friends doing? How many does he have now. I'll be he's going to be a real Casanova when he gets older. Well, he's gotta follow Mick's & my footsteps hay. I just hope they don't make some of the same mistakes I have. But another ol' saying fits in there "live and learn". Right.

I haven't received any mail for a few days nor the package you said you sent. Guess there must be too much going on around where the mail comes in from the states. Oh well, as long as it comes I'll be happy. Better late then never - Hay.

Well gang guess I'll close for now and get back to work. I feel guilty. So take good care and God bless all of you from me who loves and misses you all very, very much.

Your son & brother,
Tom

P.S. Tell Petey I said be a good boy or no plane ride. See what he has to say about that. Give him a big hug & kiss for me too. Guess thats the only way he understands that I think of him alot too.

By for now
Love
Tom
