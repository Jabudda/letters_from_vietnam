# Letter 036: notes

## Source

Three sheets of lined notebook paper (blue rules), written in **red ink** on one side, six pages ("With a red pen even?"). Pages 2 and 3 are numbered in circles and page 4 has a number cut off at the top edge; pages 1, 5 and 6 are not numbered. Each page runs on from the one before. Dated at the top right **"March 25, 1969 / Pleiku, Viet Nam"**, the place and date in his hand. Addressed "Dear Dad, Mom & Kids". Signed "Your son & brother, Tom", then a P.S. about Petey signed "By for now, Love, Tom". There is no envelope in this folder.

## Open questions for the family

1. **"Almost exactly 30 months ago I raised my right hand. And joined the army."** Thirty months before 25 March 1969 is about **25 September 1966**. Does the family know the day Dad enlisted? (Letter 008 says his unit "got here in '66 & we did in '67".)
2. **Australia.** "In a few more days I hope to be in Australia but that all depends on if I get my money orders in time." In Letter 029 (Easter, 6 April 1969, written at Cam Ranh Bay) the Australia R&R had fallen through. This letter is the plan; Letter 029 is how it turned out.
3. **"3 to 6 months left (depends on the school drop)."** The early release to start school (M.T.C., Letters 008, 019, 032, 033). His ETS later came to 21 August 1969 (Letter 026).
4. **"So ol' [possible reading: Danny] will be coming here pretty soon hay?"** Who was going to Vietnam? Letter 008 (10 May 1969) says "By now, Davey should be over here hay." Is this the same person (the reading could be "Davey")?
5. **Jim.** "I hope Jim is doing OK? Haven't heard from him in a while… lost his address." Letter 006 asks again for Jim's address and why the family never mentions him, and Letter 008 says "Jim & I were one of their first replacements" and "I sure hope he is OK?" Who was Jim, and was he all right?
6. **The plane ride.** "Tell Petey I said be a good boy or no plane ride." The same promise is in Letters 019 and 029. Did Dad and Petey ever go on that plane ride?
7. **"I walk out of Ft. Louis as a 'Mr. Tom.'"** Very likely Fort Lewis, Washington (interpretation). Was that where he was discharged?
8. **The uniform.** "Think I should come home with my uniform one [possible reading: on] - for the last time? I really don't want to but I'll leave that up to you all." Did he?
9. **The package.** "I haven't received any mail for a few days nor the package you said you sent." What was in it?
10. **Readings to check:** "[possible reading: Danny]" (page 2); "one [possible reading: on]" (page 4); "I'll be he's" (page 4); the number cut off at the top of page 4.

## Historical notes

- **Verified fact (from the letter):** He wrote it at Pleiku on 25 March 1969, on guard that night, while "suppose to be working".
- **Interpretation:** Letter 016 (4 or 5 March 1969) and this letter fall in the same month; Letter 028 (8 April) and Letter 029 (6 April) follow his trip to Cam Ranh Bay.
- **Unknown:** Whether he went to Fort Lewis; the letter says only that he expected to.
