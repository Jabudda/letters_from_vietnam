May 10, 1966 [as written; see notes]  
Viet Nam

Dear Dad, Mom & Kids,

Hi there! Got your letter & package today and sure was glad about it. I [possible reading: didn't] feel too good so went to bed this evening and fell asleep! When I woke up I thought it was the next day but it was only 7:00 at night - not 7:00 the next morning. Needless to say - I missed supper and was starving - well not really starving but sure the hell was hungry. It all tasted so good and I sure made a pig out of myself. I put some heat in a can and then gas and I cooked up the beef & gravy. It sure was good Burp!! Once again - thanks alot cause it sure was all appreciated. Oh, [possible reading: Snoopy] also. The [possible reading: Gaines] burgers I mean. I was [possible reading: dipped 'em] in in my gravy for him. He sure did like it.

Well, did you ever get over that beer party at Dave's house. Bet all of you sure had a good time. By now, Davey should be over here hay. I wonder how Jim is doing. Remember when you sent his address way back when? Well, I wrote to him and never got an answer. I sure hope he is OK? Did you hear anything on Jim yet?? I'd hate for you to call his parents (or I and find out the worst has happened. You see what I mean. I would like to drop Jim a line again but lost his address. So if you have heard anything good on him I'd like for you to send his address again. Maybe he never got my letter - which is very likely, isn't it! I'd hate, like I said before - for you to call his folks and find out something bad has happened. You know what I mean.

About that Super 8 movie camera. I'll send you a page on those in a PX catalog when I can find one. Then you can compare the prices OK? I'll look around for one tomorrow. That part where you said - "before I was discharged" really made me feel good. Mainly cause I find it hard to believe at times. Such is life hay? [possible reading: Our (my)] time will come when we'll be a complete happy family again hay.

How's ol' Gene Plummer doing? You said he lost alot of weight. Boy, I can't picture him much skinnier then he used to be. Must be about like a skelton now hay. Thats too bad but [possible reading: Im] sure all will work out for him.

About M.T.C. Yes, it will almost be a month drop. Its just the idea of feeling obligated that I don't care for. But all always works out for the best hay. Well not always - but mostly. As you said - we'll work with what we have. By the way - did you ever get my April $200 pay check?? Don't forget to tell me OK? Thanks. Cause if they start messing with me I'd like to know as soon as possible. How much does that make I have saved up now? Just curious - as usual. You know me.

Lets see - you asked how far An Khe was from me. Its about 35 miles or so. Not very far. Ive been there quite a few times. Its more so between Pleiku & Qui Nhon though. About half way. From Pleiku to Ankhe is closer to Cambodian border. We're about 28 miles at the present. What place am I near you asked. Well at present we're in Ben Het, a bit past Kontum. Our main HQ is in Nha Trang. Ive only been there for about a week all together. Just before I came here both times & for R&R (before I went) And also when I left both times from this country. I guess it equals about 9 or 10 days. I don't like the place anyways - probably cause they *try* to run it too state side which is impossible over here. I haven't been all over Viet Nam since I been here. But have been in every place possible in the Central Highlands. The history of our unit since its been here is pretty interesting and if I ever run into a copy I'll send it OK? Maybe they'll give me one before I leave. They should - Jim & I were one of their first replacements. Ours [possible reading: unit] got here in '66 & we did in '67.

Well, I guess I'll stop talking for now and go see Ken (on guard) Its his 21st birthday today. So take good care of yourselves and God bless all of you from your ol' son + brother. Who loves and misses you all more then anything in this world.

Love & miss  
Tom

P.S. Say "hi" to all - Thanks.
