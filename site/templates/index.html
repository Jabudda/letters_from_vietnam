01 / Why we’re keeping them

History has dates.
These letters have a voice.

War is more than dates, places, and battles. It is sons, brothers, friends, parents, fear, hope, and survival. These letters offer a glimpse of that human experience through one family’s correspondence.

Dad’s Vietnam Letter Archive brings together letters kept by his family, with original scans and carefully prepared transcriptions as the collection takes shape.

These letters are shared as accurately as we can make them: his spelling, his humor, his worries, just as he wrote them. We keep them as a reminder to people in the future that war does not end when a soldier comes home. Its effects reach far and last long, for the family who waited and wrote back, and for the man who went.
