# Glossary

Terms, abbreviations and slang from the letters, with the letters they appear in. Definitions need a source in `sources.md`.

| Term | Meaning | Letters | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hay | His spelling of "hey", used throughout. | 001 | Letter text |
| line skipping letter | Writing on every other ruled line; he says it is easier to write, easier to read, and makes the letter look longer. | 001 | Letter text |
| pulling guard | Standing guard duty. | 001 | Letter text |
| under strength | A unit with fewer people than it is authorized. His word is read as "under strengthed" (uncertain). | 001 | Needs a source |
