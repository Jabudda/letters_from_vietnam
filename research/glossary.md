# Glossary

Terms, abbreviations and slang from the letters, with the letters they appear in. Definitions need a source in `sources.md`.

| Term | Meaning | Letters | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hay | His spelling of "hey", used throughout. | 001, 002 | Letter text |
| line skipping letter | Writing on every other ruled line; he says it is easier to write, easier to read, and makes the letter look longer. | 001 | Letter text |
| pulling guard | Standing guard duty. | 001 | Letter text |
| under strength | A unit with fewer people than it is authorized. His word is read as "under strengthed" (uncertain). | 001 | Needs a source |
| BIG WORD | Printed in capitals above "oppertunity": he was impressing the family by using a big word, which he misspelled. Possibly also an inside joke: Grandma was always correcting the family's spelling and punctuation. | 002 | Andy (family) |
| the Oasis | Where he had been "for the last 3 or 4 days", with red dust "about ankle deep". Very likely LZ Oasis, southwest of Pleiku. | 002 | Wikipedia, "Landing Zone Oasis" |
| 2½ truck | A two-and-a-half-ton truck; he was driving one in May 1969. | 003 | Andy (family) |
| reup | Re-enlist ("I guess I'll reup for sure now! Like hell!"). | 003 | Letter text |
| Plumb. appren. | Plumbing apprenticeship, which he hoped to switch his college plan to. He did become a plumber (Andy). | 003 | Letter text; Andy |
| stay awake pills | Something the family had sent before and he asked for again; what exactly is not known. | 003 | Letter text |
| HHB 3/6 ARTY | Headquarters and Headquarters Battery, 3rd Battalion, 6th Artillery: his unit, from his return address. | 004 | Envelope; Andy (family); DTIC AD0387394 |
| bunker line | A line he fixed that had "some short in it"; very likely a field telephone wire between bunkers (interpretation). | 004 | Letter text |
| humped sand bags | Carried and filled sandbags ("got rid of my baby fat ya know"). | 004 | Letter text |
| Free | Written on the envelope where a stamp would go. | 004 | Envelope |
| ARVN | Army of the Republic of (South) Vietnam ("the ARVN's"). | 005 | Letter text |
| NVA | North Vietnamese Army. | 005 | Letter text |
| Cam. | His short form for a Cambodian soldier. | 005 | Letter text |
| CIDG | Civilian Irregular Defense Group: local, often Montagnard, soldiers trained by US Special Forces; at Ben Het they lived in the camp with their families. | 005 | Stars and Stripes 1969; ARSOF History |
| Montagnards | "Mountain people": the Central Highlands' indigenous tribes; Dad described them as warriors who brought their whole families (Andy). | 005 | ARSOF History; Andy (family) |
| newsreels | Film taken by a news crew; he says CBS was filming on their hill. | 005 | Letter text |
| THE DAY | The day he would go home, about 1,440 hours (60 days) away. | 005 | Letter text |
