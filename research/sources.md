# Sources

Every source cited in the archive, in one list. Add an entry when you first cite a source in a letter's `notes.md`.

Format: Author or organization. *Title*. Publisher or archive, date. URL or catalog reference. Accessed date.

- The Planetary Society. *Apollo 11*. Mission page with timeline (touchdown 20:17:39 UTC, 20 July 1969; splashdown 24 July 1969). https://planetary.org/space-missions/apollo-11. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 001)
- Encyclopaedia Britannica. *Apollo 11*. https://www.britannica.com/explore/space/apollo-11/. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 001)
- United Press International. *Workers strike at Schlitz brewery*. 1 June 1981. Mentions "a 37-day strike hit Schlitz, Pabst and Miller in 1969". https://www.upi.com/Archives/1981/06/01/Workers-strike-at-Schlitz-brewery/4436360216000/. Accessed 2026-11-10. Found by Andy. (Cited in: 001)
- Milwaukee Public Library Digital Collections. *Police observe Schlitz brewery strikers*. Photograph dated 17 June 1969; description notes Miller and Pabst also on strike, strike lasted five weeks. https://content.mpl.org/digital/collection/HstoricPho/id/4013/. Accessed 2026-11-10. Rights to be checked before any reuse. (Cited in: 001)
- United Press International. *Miller workers strike*. 3 June 1983. States the 1969 Miller strike lasted 37 days and also affected Pabst and Schlitz. https://www.upi.com/Archives/1983/06/03/Miller-workers-strike/7724423460800/. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 001)
- Good Beer Hunting. *More Than Just Free Beer: The Labor, Struggles, and Activism of Pre-Prohibition Brewery Workers in Milwaukee*. 10 Feb 2021. Describes the 1969 strike as 36 days during a hot summer. https://www.goodbeerhunting.com/blog/2021/2/10/more-than-just-free-beer-the-labor-struggles-and-activism-of-pre-prohibition-brewery-workers-in-milwaukee. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 001)
- Wikipedia. *Apollo 11*. Landing 20:17:40 UTC, 20 July 1969; moonwalk broadcast live to an estimated 600 million viewers; splashdown 24 July 1969. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_11. Accessed 2026-11-10. Supplied by Andy. (Cited in: 001)
- Wikipedia. *Landing Zone Oasis*. Base about 24 km southwest of Pleiku, used 1966–71; later by 3rd Brigade, 4th Infantry Division; laterite airstrip. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landing_Zone_Oasis. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 002, 003)
- Local Vietnam. *Pleiku – Travel Guide & Things To Do*. General climate summary (dry season about November–April). https://localvietnam.com/?p=93626. Accessed 2026-11-10. Modern travel source. (Cited in: 002)
- War-Stories.com. *LZ Oasis: Mother's Day POWs*. Veteran's account (B Co., 704th Maintenance Bn.) of the attack on LZ Oasis, 11 May 1969. https://www.war-stories.com/lz-oasis-reynolds-1969.htm. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 003)
- The Mighty Ninth (9th Artillery veterans). *Happy Mother's Day*. Veterans' accounts of the LZ Oasis attack, 11 May 1969. https://themightyninth.sdsu.edu/War%20Stories/Happy%20Mothers%20Day/Happy%20Mother's%20Day.htm. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 003)
- The Mighty Ninth. *Operation Western Pistol*. Notes that LZ Oasis came under ground attack on the night of 10 May 1969. https://themightyninth.sdsu.edu/War%20Stories/Opn%20West%20Pistol/operation_western_pistol.htm. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 003)
- 3d Battalion, 6th Artillery. *Operational Report – Lessons Learned for Quarterly Period Ending 31 October 1967*. 4 Nov 1967. Battalion organization; headquarters at APO 96318. Defense Technical Information Center AD0387394. https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/pdfs/AD0387394.pdf. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 004)
- Mike's Research. *M108 and M109 Self-Propelled Howitzers in Vietnam*. The 3/6th Artillery was based at Camp Saint Barbara (later Artillery Hill) outside Pleiku, in the 52nd Artillery Group, I Field Force. https://mikesresearch.com/2025/12/30/m108-and-m109-self-propelled-howitzers-in-vietnam. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 004)
- 1/92nd Field Artillery Association. *The Organization of the Field Artillery in Vietnam*. David Powell. 3rd Bn, 6th Arty (105 SP) in the 52nd Artillery Group, 1968–69. https://www.bravecannons.org/the_gun/unit_org.html. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 004)
- 1/92nd Field Artillery Association. *Operational Report, 1st Battalion, 92d Artillery, Nov 1968 – Jan 1969*. Artillery Hill, Pleiku; batteries working with the 3rd Battalion, 6th Artillery. https://www.bravecannons.org/History/ORLL/h_nov_jan_69.html. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 004)
- US Department of Veterans Affairs, Veterans Legacy Memorial. *Peter F Jafuta*. US Army, World War II, TEC 4. https://www.vlm.cem.va.gov/PETERFJAFUTA/35385A. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 004)
- US Department of Veterans Affairs, National Center for PTSD. *History of PTSD in Veterans: Civil War to DSM-5*. Matthew J. Friedman. Earlier names (nostalgia, soldier's heart, shell shock, battle fatigue); PTSD added to DSM-III in 1980. https://www.ptsd.va.gov/understand/what/history_ptsd.asp. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 004)
- BrainFacts.org (Society for Neuroscience). *The Changing Face of Posttraumatic Stress Disorder*. Alexis Wnuk, 20 Jun 2019. No official trauma diagnosis between 1968 and 1980; Vietnam veterans pushed for one. https://www.brainfacts.org/diseases-and-disorders/mental-health/2019/the-changing-face-of-posttraumatic-stress-disorder-062019. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 004)
- The National WWII Museum. *The Last Days of Dachau Concentration Camp*. Jason Dawsey, 15 Jul 2022. Liberation on 29 April 1945; liberating units; the death train. https://www.nationalww2museum.org/war/articles/last-days-dachau-concentration-camp. Accessed 2026-11-10. Supplied by Andy. (Cited in: 004)
- US Holocaust Memorial Museum, Holocaust Encyclopedia. *The 45th Infantry Division*. Recognized as a liberating unit of Dachau in 1985. https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/the-45th-infantry-division. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 004)
- US Holocaust Memorial Museum, Holocaust Encyclopedia. *Honoring US Army Divisions as Liberators of Nazi Camps*. Divisions recognized from unit records, including those arriving within 48 hours. https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/us-army-units. Accessed 2026-11-10. (Cited in: 004)
