# Research

Historical research shared across letters. Every claim is labeled:

- **Verified fact**: supported by the letters or by reliable documentary evidence.
- **Historical context**: events or conditions documented independently of the letters.
- **Interpretation**: reasonable analysis that is not definitively established.
- **Unknown**: can't currently be verified.

Never place the writer at a battle or operation just because it happened near the date or place of a letter.

| File | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `sources.md` | Bibliography: every source cited anywhere in the archive. |
| `glossary.md` | Military terms, abbreviations and slang from the letters. |
| `timeline.md` | Dated events from the letters, alongside sourced historical context. |
| `people-and-places.md` | Index of people and places mentioned, with the letters they appear in. |

Preferred sources: the National Archives (NARA), Library of Congress, U.S. Army Center of Military History, unit histories and veterans' associations, the Vietnam Veterans Memorial Fund, and public-domain U.S. Army Map Service maps of the period.
