# Keep your own family’s letters

Somewhere, in a closet or a shoebox, there may be letters like these in your family too. They are worth keeping.

- **Store them flat and dark,** away from heat, damp and sunlight, in folders or boxes that are acid-free if you can get them.
- **Scan or photograph every page,** envelopes too, front and back, and keep the files in more than one place.
- **Write down what you know** while the people who remember are still here to ask: who is named, where it was written, what happened next.
- **Copy the words exactly,** spelling and all. The mistakes are part of the voice.

A letter takes a few minutes to write. Kept, it can last a lifetime and longer.
