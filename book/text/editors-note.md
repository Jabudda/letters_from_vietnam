# A note on this book

**What is here.** Dad’s letters home, kept by his family, are printed here in the order he wrote them. They come from both of his tours in Vietnam. Many are dated only by day and month; where the year is our best reading rather than his, the notes at the back say so, and some letters may belong to a different tour than the one we have placed them in.

**His words, as he wrote them.** Each letter appears in a *reading edition*. It follows the page word for word. The only changes are paragraph breaks, joining words split at the end of a line, and putting in their place the words he wrote above the line. His spelling, grammar, slang and humor are kept exactly: *hay*, *alot*, *nite*, *Im*, *HA*. Where a word can’t be read with certainty it is marked, for example *[unclear]* or *[possible reading: …]*. Nothing has been guessed silently.

**Privacy.** The family’s home street address and ZIP code are left out of the text and covered in the pictures of the envelopes.

**History, kept separate.** His letters are not interrupted. What we have learned about the places and events he mentions is gathered at the back of the book in *Notes on the letters*, with sources. Each note says what kind of statement it is: a fact from the letter or from a record, historical context, our interpretation, or unknown. A letter written near a battle does not mean he was in it, and we have tried never to say more than the evidence does.

**The family’s voice.** Under some letters are notes *From the family*: Andy’s memories of what Dad told him and what the letters meant at home.

**The archive.** Every page was scanned and every transcription checked by the family. An exact, line-by-line transcription of each page, the scans and the full record of corrections are kept in the family’s archive and on the website, *Letters From Vietnam* (jabudda.github.io/letters_from_vietnam).
