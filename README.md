# Letters From Vietnam

**A family archive of letters home, preserved for remembrance.**

This repository holds the working archive for a free commemorative book and website built from my father's handwritten letters, sent home from Vietnam in the late 1960s. The aim is to keep his words exactly as he wrote them, set them in their historical context, and share them with family and with anyone interested in the people who served and the families who waited at home.

> *This book is not for sale. It is for remembrance.*

## What is here

| Folder | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| [`archive/`](archive/) | The archival record: one folder per letter (`archive/letters/001` … `047`) with its metadata, transcriptions and research notes, plus the master inventory and checksums for the original scans. |

| [`research/`](research/) | Historical research shared across letters: sources, glossary, timeline, people and places. |

## Where the original scans are

The full-resolution scans (about 10 GB: 274 files covering 47 letters) are **not** stored in this repository. The masters are kept by the family in Google Drive, with a second copy on disk. [`archive/checksums-sha256.txt`](archive/checksums-sha256.txt) records a SHA-256 fingerprint of every master file, so any copy can be checked against the originals at any time. See [`docs/scan-naming.md`](docs/scan-naming.md).

Only reduced, privacy-reviewed web images are published here, and only for letters whose transcriptions have been approved by the family.

## Principles

- **Preserve their words.** Transcriptions keep the writer's spelling, punctuation, slang and humor. Nothing is silently corrected, polished or dramatized.
- **Mark what is uncertain.** Hard-to-read words are flagged (`[unclear]`, `[illegible]`, `[possible reading: …]`) rather than guessed.
- **Keep history separate from the letters.** Historical context is sourced, and labeled as verified fact, context, interpretation or unknown. No one is placed at an event just because it happened nearby.
- **Family review comes first.** A letter is published only after its transcription has been reviewed and approved by the family.
- **Respect privacy.** Home addresses, phone numbers and personal identifiers on envelopes are blurred in published images.
- **Label anything generated.** Any illustration is marked *AI-generated historical interpretation — not an original photograph.*

## Status

Archive set up November 2026. All 47 letters are inventoried and their master scans checksummed; nine have earlier draft transcriptions awaiting verification. See [`archive/inventory.csv`](archive/inventory.csv) for the status of each letter.
